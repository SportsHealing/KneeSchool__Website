One library, read at seven depths

Each page carries the same topic at every tier the syllabus asks for, so a patient and a medical student read the same article and open it at a different depth.

Coverage. Section 1 Knee Fundamentals is complete: sixteen pages, each passing the style gate. All sixteen are editorial drafts. None has been evidence verified and none has been through consultant review.
