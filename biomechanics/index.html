The Biomechanics Academy

Section 3 · Forces, movement and what they explain

Seventeen chapters on how the knee moves and what loads it, written from school age to consultant level. 114 of 188 pages are published; the rest are written in syllabus order.

How to read a number on these pages

Biomechanics is a field where three credible methods give three different answers to the same question, and the difference is usually the method rather than the knee.

  • Every figure carries its method. A cadaver figure, a laboratory figure and an in vivo figure are three different kinds of number. Each page says which it is quoting, because a figure without its method attached cannot be used.
  • Every figure is unchecked. No measurement on this site has been verified against a source. Each is registered with the sentence it supports and a verification state, so the list can be worked through.
  • How It Is Measured is a section, not an aside. Each page has a heading for the methods and their limits, because a template with nowhere to say so produces pages that quote figures as settled. See decision 014.
  • The landmark papers pages are held. Every chapter ends with a page about named studies. This build cannot retrieve a paper and will not invent a citation, so those eleven pages wait. See decision 014.
