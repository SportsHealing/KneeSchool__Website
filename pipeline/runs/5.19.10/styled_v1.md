# Future Applications

The effect may end as one route to a general capability rather than as a
specialty of its own.

## For Medical Students

### What It Shows

Several newer methods aim at the same tissue property this effect is sensitive
to, which is the organisation of collagen. 5.18.1 covers quantitative MRI.

If one of them measures that property without needing the angle controlled, the
orientation route becomes a historical step rather than a tool. 5.18.4 covers UTE
imaging.

It matters because the chapter's subject could be absorbed into general
quantitative imaging, and the lesson it teaches about reading images would
outlast it. 5.1.8 covers the future of knee imaging.

### How It Is Acquired

Three directions would each change what this chapter is for.

Sequences that image ordered collagen directly reach the tissue the standard
ones cannot, which removes the need to exploit an artefact. 5.18.4 covers UTE
imaging.

Quantitative mapping with orientation handled explicitly would turn the effect
from a confound into a term in a calculation. 5.18.2 covers T2 mapping.

Automated reading could flag an orientation appearance before a human reports
it, which addresses the error rather than the measurement. 5.18.8 covers
artificial intelligence in imaging.

Positioning systems that set the angle deliberately would make the measurement
possible without new physics. 5.18.5 covers weight-bearing MRI.

### Key Learning Points

- Several newer methods aim at the same tissue property.
- One of them may measure it without the angle being controlled.
- The orientation route could become a historical step.
- Automated reading addresses the error rather than the measurement.
- Deliberate positioning needs no new physics.

### How Good It Is

A page about the future is the easiest page in a section to write badly, and the
safeguard is to describe directions rather than outcomes.

Its best contribution is the observation that the chapter's subject may be
superseded, which is unusual for a named effect and worth saying plainly. 5.2.20
covers the magic angle effect.

It is limited because no claim here is dated, ranked or sourced, and the chapter
does not know which direction will matter. 5.1.7 covers evidence-based imaging.

It is limited again because automated reading would reduce the error without
touching the measurement, so progress on one side would not be progress on the
other. 5.18.8 covers artificial intelligence in imaging.

## FRCS Level

### How Good It Is

At decision level nothing on this page should change a practice today, and that
is the correct reading of it.

The habit worth adopting now is the confirming sequence, which is available and
free. 5.2.14 covers T2 imaging.

The habit worth adopting next is recording position on quantitative work, which
costs a template field. 5.20.1 covers structured reporting.

Everything beyond those two is a research programme, and a service should not be
built on it yet. 5.19.8 covers the Imperial magic angle programme.

A department choosing between advanced sequences for cartilage work should choose
on the evidence for each, which this chapter cannot supply. 5.19.6 covers the
cartilage applications.

### Where It Misleads

The commonest error with a future page is reading a direction as a plan. 5.1.8
covers the future of knee imaging.

An advanced sequence is bought for a question it has not been shown to answer.
5.18.3 covers T1 rho imaging.

Automated reading is trusted to catch an error its training never contained.
5.18.8 covers artificial intelligence in imaging.

A measurement is reported to a patient before it has an outcome attached. 5.1.7
covers evidence-based imaging.

The chapter's own enthusiasm is mistaken for evidence, which is the risk a
chapter devoted to one effect carries throughout. 5.19.7 covers the current
research.

### Key Learning Points

- A direction is read as a plan.
- A sequence is bought for a question it has not answered.
- Automated reading is trusted beyond its training.
- A measurement reaches a patient before an outcome is attached.
- A chapter devoted to one effect risks reading as advocacy.

## Fellowship Level

### Where It Misleads

At fellowship level the closing position of this chapter is that its subject is
smaller than its name and its lesson is larger.

The effect itself is one relaxation mechanism in one tissue class, which does not
warrant an academy, and the chapter has been written to be useful rather than to
justify its own heading. 5.19.1 covers the physics of magic angle imaging.

What generalises is the habit of asking what the instrument contributed to an
appearance before believing the appearance, which the physics chapter states as
its own conclusion and this chapter demonstrates ten times over. 5.2.20 covers
the magic angle effect.

What also generalises is the chapter's rule, now stated as a general one: a
measurement whose governing variable is not recorded is not a measurement.
5.19.9 covers the clinical translation.

The work this chapter needs is the work the whole section needs, which is source
access and a reviewer, and until it arrives nothing here carries more authority
than careful standard teaching. 5.1.7 covers evidence-based imaging.

### Key Learning Points

- The subject is smaller than its name and the lesson is larger.
- One relaxation mechanism in one tissue class does not warrant an academy.
- Ask what the instrument contributed before believing the appearance.
- A measurement whose governing variable is unrecorded is not a measurement.
- Nothing here carries more authority than careful standard teaching.
