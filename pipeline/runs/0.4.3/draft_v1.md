# UCAT and Admissions Tests

What admissions tests for medicine are, what they test, and where the current
rules are published. This page is orientation only and gives no coaching.

## For Young Learners

### What This Is

Most United Kingdom medical schools ask applicants to sit an admissions test
in addition to their school examinations. The UCAT is the one used by the
large majority.

It is not a knowledge test. There is no syllabus of facts to learn. It tests
how quickly and accurately you handle information under time pressure, which
is a different thing and is why it feels unfamiliar.

### Why It Matters

Schools use admissions tests to separate applicants whose predicted grades
look similar, which by that stage is most of them.

How much weight a school puts on the score varies enormously. Some use it as a
threshold, some score it alongside everything else, some barely use it. That
variation is published by each school and is worth knowing before you apply.

### How It Works

The UCAT is sat on a computer at a test centre, usually in the summer before
you apply. It is made up of several timed sections covering verbal reasoning,
decision making, quantitative reasoning and a situational judgement section.

The number of sections, their names, the timings and how results are reported
have all changed over the years and will change again. The UCAT Consortium
publishes the current structure, and that is the only version worth reading.

A small number of courses use a different test. Which test a course requires
is listed on the course's own page.

### How to Prepare

This site does not provide test preparation, question banks or coaching, and
that is a deliberate limit rather than an oversight. The platform teaches knee
medicine.

What can be said safely is this. The test consortium publishes free official
practice material and a full practice test, and candidates consistently report
that familiarity with the format matters more than anything else.

Use the official free material first. Decide after that whether you need
anything more.

### Common Mistakes

Treating it as a knowledge examination and trying to revise content for it.

Sitting it without having seen the format. The timing is the hardest part and
it is the part free practice material fixes.

Assuming a score means the same thing everywhere. The same score can be
decisive at one school and nearly irrelevant at another.

Reading last year's rules. The format has changed more than once.

### Where to Find Out More

The UCAT Consortium publishes the test structure, the booking process, the
fees, the bursary scheme and the official free practice material.

Each medical school publishes how it uses the score, which varies more than
most applicants expect.

The UCAT bursary covers the test fee for eligible candidates, and the
consortium publishes the eligibility rules. Anyone who thinks the cost is a
barrier should read that page before ruling the test out.

### Key Learning Points

- Admissions tests for medicine test information handling under time pressure, not recall.
- The UCAT is used by most United Kingdom medical schools; a few courses use another test.
- How much a score counts differs sharply between schools and each one publishes its method.
- Official free practice material exists, and format familiarity matters more than anything else.
- A bursary covers the fee for eligible candidates.

If any of this sounds like something happening to you, speak to a parent,
teacher, coach or doctor.
