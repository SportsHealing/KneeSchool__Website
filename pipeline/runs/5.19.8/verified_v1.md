### How It Is Acquired

A research programme on this subject would be built from a small number of
identifiable parts, and naming them lets a reader judge the real one when they
reach it.

A sequence development strand, because using orientation as a measurement needs
acquisition that controls it. 5.2.21 covers the advanced MRI techniques.

A positioning strand, because the angle has to be set deliberately rather than
accepted from the couch. 5.18.5 covers weight-bearing MRI.

A validation strand, comparing the imaging measure against tissue examined
directly. 5.19.2 covers the collagen architecture.

A clinical strand, asking whether the measure changes a decision rather than
whether it correlates with one. 5.19.9 covers the clinical translation.
