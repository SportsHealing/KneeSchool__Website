## Why a decision was needed

Part 1 governs tier coverage and page scope. On Section 5 it says one thing:

> **Section 5: Imaging Academy** (estimated 250 to 350 pages). Physics,
> radiographs, ultrasound, MRI protocols and normal anatomy, then structure
> specific MRI (5.6 meniscal to 5.12 patellofemoral), bone and marrow, synovial
> disorders, paediatric MRI, CT, post operative imaging, advanced imaging, the
> Magic Angle MRI Academy (5.19), reporting (5.20) and landmark papers (5.21).

Part 2, which would carry the real page map, is not in this repository. This is
the position Section 4 was in, and decision 024 is the precedent.

## What the outline got wrong

The outline file recorded 161 Section 5 pages. Chapters 5.2 to 5.5 are organised
as navigational sub sections in the source:

```
Chapter 5.2 Imaging Physics for Knee Specialists
Section 5.2.1 X-ray Physics
5.2.1.1 Generation of X-rays
5.2.1.2 Image Formation
```

The outline took `Section 5.2.1 X-ray Physics` as a page and dropped the five
leaves beneath it. Across the four chapters that lost 78 pages and invented 15.

Part 1's numbering convention says navigational groupings never appear in page
ids, which is the ruling decision 024 applied to chapter 4.2. So those leaves
are renumbered flat within their chapter and the headings become no page at all.
Section 5 is **224 pages**, which also sits inside Part 1's own estimate where
161 did not.

## What is derived, and from what

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| Page ids and titles | The source document's own lines |
| `must_not_cover` | Part 1's authority boundaries, inverted for an imaging section |
| `deeper_pages` | Part 1's Spiral Linkage Map, per page where it names a page and per chapter where it names a chapter, falling back to the Imaging row's own deeper step |
| `priority` | The spiral map: a chapter on a core topic chain is high, a chapter reached only through the Imaging row is medium, landmark papers low |
| `scope` | The title restated, with the clause chosen by what the chapter is for |
| `page_type` | `imaging`, or `landmark_papers` for 5.21 (decision 028) |

### A trap the renumbering set

The spiral map's Osteoarthritis row reads `... 4.18 → 5.3.4 → 6.71 to 6.80 ...`.
Its `5.3.4` is the navigational heading **Osteoarthritis Imaging**. After
flattening, `5.3.4` is the page **Tunnel View**. Taking the reference at face
value wired the osteoarthritis chain to a radiographic view and left the five
real osteoarthritis radiography pages with no deeper link.

The extractor translates each old navigational id to the pages that came from
under it, so `6.71` now lands on 5.3.16 to 5.3.20. A test holds it.

## The weakest part, named rather than smoothed over

**Section 5 has no tier statement anywhere.** Decision 024 had a sentence to
lean on: Part 1's Section 4 line says "Examination for Medical Student to
Fellowship tiers". Part 1's Section 5 line says nothing about tiers at all, so
this is a weaker derivation than decision 024's and should be read that way.

What supports `medical_student, mrcs, frcs, fellowship`:

- Every page in the architecture that defers to Section 5 does so from a
  professional tier. The Section 2 chapters excluding "Imaging interpretation
  beyond normal anatomy (owned by Section 5)" run medical_student to frcs.
- Section 4 defers its imaging to Section 5 the same way and runs
  medical_student to fellowship.
- Part 1 section 3 says every tier set in Part 1 is an editorial default
  awaiting Editor in Chief sign off. This one has not even that standing.

What argues against applying one set to all 224 pages: 5.2 physics is
foundational and might reach further down, while 5.19 Magic Angle and 5.20
Reporting are plainly advanced and may not belong at medical student level at
all. A per chapter tier set is the thing Part 2 would settle.

## Why the extractor is committed

`pages_4.json` was generated by a script in a scratchpad directory. The
container was reclaimed and the script went with it. The committed map survived
and the thing that made it did not, so Section 4's map can no longer be
re-derived or checked against the source.

`tools/extract_section5.py` is in the repository for that reason, and
`Section5PageMapIsRebuildable` holds the committed map against a fresh rebuild.

## Cost

The same cost decision 024 carried. 224 pages will be written against a map this
process built, and if Part 2 arrives and disagrees, the tier sets and the
boundary lists are what will need changing rather than the prose. Writing an
extractor rather than a hand list is what keeps that a regeneration rather than
a rewrite.
