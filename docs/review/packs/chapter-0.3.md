## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**0.3.1 The Road to Knee Surgeon**

- Qualifying as a doctor and qualifying as a surgeon are two different things, several years apart.
- The route is a fixed sequence of stages, each with its own entry point and its own assessment.
- MRCS sits early in surgical training and FRCS sits near the end of it.
- Fellowship is the stage where a general orthopaedic surgeon becomes a knee surgeon.
- Most jobs in knee care are not surgical jobs.

**0.3.2 A Day in the Life**

- A job title tells you less about a career than a description of the week does.
- Most knee problems are managed without an operation, so most knee care is not surgery.
- Every role in a knee service involves explaining things to worried people.
- The five roles described reward noticeably different strengths.

**0.3.3 Beyond Surgery**

- Most of the work in knee health is done by people who do not operate.
- These roles divide into hands on care, measurement and design.
- Several of the non medical routes are as competitive as medicine.
- Entry requirements are published by each profession's own body and change regularly.

**0.3.4 What MRCS and FRCS Actually Are**

- MRCS and FRCS are examinations taken while working, not university degrees.
- MRCS sits early in surgical training and is the same for every surgical specialty.
- FRCS sits near the end of training and is specific to trauma and orthopaedics.
- Both examinations test whether a candidate can justify a decision out loud, not just recall facts.
- Anatomy is the subject that carries through both.

**0.3.5 Women in Orthopaedics**

- Orthopaedic surgery has one of the smallest proportions of women of any surgical specialty.
- The commonly offered explanations are exposure, role models and working patterns, not ability.
- The physical demands of the work come from positioning and instruments rather than strength.
- The current figures are published annually and should be read from the source.

**0.3.6 Ask a Surgeon**

- Every question is read and checked by a person before any answer is published.
- The archive is not open yet, and this page says so rather than implying otherwise.
- Questions about how something works can be answered; questions about your own knee cannot.
- Never include identifying personal details in a question.
