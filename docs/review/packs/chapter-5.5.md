## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.5.1 Standard Protocol**

- Protocols differ between departments and the report does not say how.
- Two reports on the same knee are not comparable.
- The grading conventions were derived on particular weightings.
- Isotropic acquisition and acceleration both change the images again.
- State the question on the request and ask for the protocol when it matters.

**5.5.2 Sports Knee Protocol**

- The detection gap is established and the protocol changes it.
- The limiting factor is the request and availability, not knowledge.
- A better acquisition feeds a weakly defined partial tear diagnosis.
- Protocol content varies under the same name.
- Name the suspected lesion rather than the protocol.

**5.5.3 Post-operative Protocol**

- The protocol cannot be selected without knowing the implant.
- A clinical communication failure becomes an imaging failure.
- Artefact reduction has advanced unevenly and is not reported.
- Those techniques alter the bone the measurements are made on.
- Naming the implant is the cheapest improvement and the least often done.

**5.5.4 Paediatric Protocol**

- The normal reference is a trajectory rather than a single standard.
- No report states the age dependent expectation used.
- Red marrow variation widens the normal in the population imaged for sport.
- Measuring the proportion of physis involved is not reliable routinely.
- Automated reading is trained predominantly on adult knees.

**5.5.5 Tumour Protocol**

- A study adequate for the knee and inadequate for the lesion is worse than none.
- A biopsy route chosen without staging can enlarge the operation.
- Most incidental marrow abnormality is not tumour.
- Advanced characterisation does not replace histology.
- The imaging's job is a well informed referral.

**5.5.6 Bones**

- Marrow oedema is found in asymptomatic and nearly all symptomatic knees.
- Population level associations are applied to individuals.
- The normal red marrow boundary is wide and moves.
- Distribution and symmetry rather than signal are the discriminators.
- A qualitative finding is used quantitatively.

**5.5.7 Cartilage**

- Morphology detects a defect and says nothing about mechanical state.
- Compositional sequences do not transfer between sequences and field strengths.
- Grading systems were derived on particular sequences.
- Cartilage is measured unloaded and deforms under load.
- The only modality that images cartilage images its shape.

**5.5.8 Menisci**

- The imaging performs best where management is least ambiguous.
- Intermediate signal in an older meniscus has no agreed meaning.
- That population is where a report most often leads to an unsupported intervention.
- The ambiguity is partly a protocol artefact.
- Name the imitators excluded, report intermediate as intermediate, measure extrusion.

**5.5.9 Ligaments**

- A continuous ligament can be functionally lax.
- Normal ligament signal has no agreed definition.
- The least reproducible call is a definitional problem.
- Knee position is not recorded and cannot be corrected for.
- Isotropic acquisition removes the oblique plane problem.

**5.5.10 Tendons**

- The two modalities are complementary and treated as alternatives.
- Both have an orientation dependent false positive from different physics.
- Both are confounded by the appearance occurring without symptoms.
- Ultra short echo time imaging changes the problem rather than removing it.
- Ultrasound for an isolated tendon, this modality when anything else is in question.

**5.5.11 Synovium**

- The answering study needs contrast the routine protocol omits.
- The request rarely says a synovial question is being asked.
- Synovitis occurs in mechanically driven arthritis too.
- Quantitative synovial measurement is not routine.
- Ultrasound with Doppler assesses activity and is rarely used alongside.

**5.5.12 Bursa**

- Sensitivity produces a list rather than a diagnosis.
- Each reported item imposes a cost without a benefit.
- The popliteal cyst's cause is the actual finding and is often omitted.
- Ultrasound is the better modality for an isolated bursal question.
- Say explicitly when fluid is within normal limits.

**5.5.13 Neurovascular Structures**

- They are in every field of view and outside almost every question.
- They are reviewed by habit rather than by protocol, and habits vary.
- Low frequency and high consequence is the worst combination for habit.
- A mandatory report field would close the gap and no template has one.
- What a study does not show is usually a choice rather than a limit.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.5.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
