## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.12.1 Trochlear Dysplasia**

- Several grading systems are in use and are not interchangeable.
- The strongest predictor is among the least reproducible measurements.
- A routine measurement takes one section through a three dimensional shape.
- Thresholds and measurements often come from different tissues.
- Name the system, the level and the surface measured.

**5.12.2 Patella Alta**

- The same patella is normal by one convention and abnormal by another.
- Reports usually give a number without its method.
- The modality changes the answer as well as the convention.
- A measurement is comparable only with one made at the same flexion angle.
- Applying a threshold to a differently measured value is routine practice.

**5.12.3 Patellar Tilt**

- Every static tilt measurement is made where the patella is safe.
- The same objection applies to the skyline view, so the posture is the problem.
- A tilt value needs both its methods stated.
- Dynamic measurement through the range is not routine.
- An isolated flexed tilt value is weak evidence.

**5.12.4 Patellar Subluxation**

- Every available study images the patella flexed, still and unloaded.
- The clinical question is never actually asked.
- Imaging supplies a past record and morphological risk factors.
- The three dynamic options fail differently and none is routine.
- The examination is more informative about the behaviour than any image.

**5.12.5 MPFL Injury**

- The site of failure is reported more confidently than the images support.
- A reconstruction can be planned for a structure never shown to be failing.
- The immature femoral attachment sits near the growth plate.
- Serial scans across episodes compare timing rather than damage.
- The report should state the interval since the episode.

**5.12.6 Cartilage Injury**

- The imaging grade and the arthroscopic grade disagree often.
- Quantitative measures vary between departments.
- A repair looks abnormal long after it has worked.
- Automated grading carries this joint's difficulty forward.
- The report should name depth, facet, area, bone and system.

**5.12.7 Patellofemoral Arthritis**

- The compartment pattern decides more than the severity.
- Comparing skylines across angles measures positioning.
- Secondary wear after instability looks like primary wear.
- The operated joint is read as progression.
- The report should name the flexion angle.

**5.12.8 Post-operative Imaging**

- The technique used is rarely identifiable from the images.
- A procedure on the groove makes later measurements describe the operation.
- A tubercle transfer moves the reference point for serial comparison.
- Cartilage near metal is the least reliable part of the study.
- The report should state what the artefact prevented.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.12.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
