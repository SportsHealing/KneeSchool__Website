## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.15.1 Normal Paediatric Anatomy**

- Stage is not age and the films settle it.
- Over-calling and under-calling do not cost the same thing.
- One study of a growing knee is a frame from a sequence.
- The interval is part of the investigation rather than a delay.
- A useful report says what it is not calling.

**5.15.2 Growth Plates**

- The physis is why paediatric practice differs from adult practice.
- Imaging contributes location rather than prognosis on the day.
- A report implying it can predict arrest is hard to correct later.
- The plate's geometry constrains the operation.
- The first report's job is to make the later comparison possible.

**5.15.3 OCD**

- One region produces both the commonest over-call and a missed diagnosis.
- None of the discriminators is a measurement.
- The child's lesion is reported in the adult lesion's vocabulary.
- Healing and static lesions differ across two studies rather than on one.
- The report says which appearances it regards as developmental.

**5.15.4 Discoid Meniscus**

- The appearance is usually right and the wording drives the referral.
- Naming the shape and stopping invites an inference.
- The tissue is irreplaceable, so an operation on signal costs the meniscus.
- A symptomatic knee with an untorn discoid meniscus is the hardest version.
- The scan excludes rather than explains the symptom.

**5.15.5 Paediatric ACL Injury**

- The structure that failed and the structure at risk can differ.
- A report organised around the ligament can omit the plate.
- The pattern shifts with maturity rather than at a point.
- Two children of similar age can sustain different injuries.
- The meniscus is irreplaceable and the knee has decades to use it.

**5.15.6 Patellar Instability**

- The numbers do not transfer from the mature knee.
- A measurement without its stage invites the wrong comparison.
- Shape develops in response to use.
- A report implying a fixed deformity overstates one study.
- The contribution is often excluding a fragment rather than explaining the knee.

**5.15.7 Developmental Variants**

- Paediatric knee imaging is mostly the discipline of not reporting things.
- The structures are better seen in a child than in an adult.
- The difficulty is interpretive rather than technical.
- The cheaper error is the one that gets over-corrected.
- A report on a growing knee describes a moment in a sequence.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.15.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
