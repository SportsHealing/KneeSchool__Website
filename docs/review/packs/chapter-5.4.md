## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.4.1 Anterior Knee**

- The resolution advantage is unavailable to anyone who was not scanning.
- A negative is a statement about one examination.
- Findings here are common in asymptomatic tendons.
- Video capture and a structured template would help and are not standard.
- Treat the study as a consultation with the person who performed it.

**5.4.2 Medial Knee**

- Layer grading is better here than on a static study.
- The injury rarely occurs in isolation, so the better tool answers less.
- The meniscocapsular junction is visible and the comparison is unstudied.
- Dynamic valgus assessment has no agreed protocol or measurement.
- Use it for a focused question in an otherwise accounted for knee.

**5.4.3 Lateral Knee**

- The nerve is the clearest resolution advantage and rarely the reason requested.
- The corner matters most functionally and cannot be completed here.
- Operator dependence is higher here than anywhere else in the knee.
- A negative lateral ultrasound is the weakest negative in knee ultrasound.
- Use it for the nerve and a named structure, not for the corner.

**5.4.4 Posterior Knee**

- The cyst is characterised definitively and its cause is invisible.
- Treating the visible thing invites recurrence.
- The vascular differential is the one urgent knee ultrasound finding.
- It depends on applying Doppler to a presumed cyst.
- This is a focused characterisation study rather than a regional assessment.

**5.4.5 Quadriceps Tendon**

- The layer level description rarely appears in a report.
- Distinguishing high grade partial from complete has no established criterion.
- The dynamic contraction test is not recorded transferably.
- Published accuracy reflects the operators in the study.
- Name the layers, state whether a contraction transmitted, and say who scanned.

**5.4.6 Patellar Tendon**

- The appearance is common in asymptomatic athletic tendons.
- Its presence shifts the diagnostic probability very little.
- Its predictive performance as a screening finding is unestablished.
- Vascularity and symptoms correlate inconsistently across studies.
- Elastography is closer to the mechanical question and is not routine.

**5.4.7 Hamstring Tendons**

- The step from tendon measurement to graft diameter involves the harvest.
- It is a screening tool rather than a planning measurement.
- Regeneration is described and its mechanical contribution is not established.
- Magnetic resonance imaging gives the same measurement within a planned study.
- Measure where graft adequacy is genuinely in doubt.

**5.4.8 Popliteus Tendon**

- The intra-articular course is a physical barrier, not a technique problem.
- A reader cannot tell from a normal report how much was seen.
- Two wordings describe the same examination and license different conclusions.
- The popliteus complex is central to posterolateral restraint.
- Report what was accessible and name what was not.

**5.4.9 MCL**

- Grades from one modality are mixed with thresholds from another.
- Dynamic valgus assessment has no protocol and no force measurement.
- Stress ultrasound would quantify it and is not in routine use.
- The best graded structure is the least decisive part of the question.
- Use it for a focused question in an accounted for knee.

**5.4.10 LCL**

- The cord is imaged end to end better than anything else laterally.
- The decision rests on the corner's rotational function.
- A confident assessment of the wrong variable is a subtle failure.
- Dynamic varus assessment is available and unquantified.
- Use it for a named structure and put the function to the examination.

**5.4.11 MPFL**

- Instability surgery is planned from bony measurements.
- The ligament is commonly abnormal after a previous dislocation.
- Dynamic assessment can be observed without being quantified.
- Dynamic magnetic resonance imaging quantifies tracking and is not routine.
- The structure is described here and the decision lives elsewhere.

**5.4.12 PLC Structures**

- Coverage is inversely related to the question's importance here.
- A report naming the corner implies a completeness never achieved.
- The nerve finding belongs to a different question and is found incidentally.
- A negative corner study of either kind carries less weight than a positive one.
- Request a named structure, and take the corner to magnetic resonance imaging.

**5.4.13 Snapping Tendons**

- The observation exists only during the examination.
- Video capture would make it transmissible and is not standard.
- A unique capability is recorded in the least reliable form.
- The condition is uncommon, so experience is thin.
- The reference standard is the same examination, so performance is unestablished.

**5.4.14 Patellar Tracking**

- The static views image the joint in its stable position.
- Ultrasound observes the unstable range without quantifying it.
- Dynamic magnetic resonance imaging quantifies it under artificial loading.
- Probe pressure and window perturb what is measured.
- Functional loading is absent from all the options.

**5.4.15 Dynamic Instability**

- Section 5 repeatedly concludes that function is not measured.
- This examination observes function and is the least standardised thing in it.
- Stress ultrasound with a measured force is described and not routine.
- The observation does not survive the examination.
- The most promising underdeveloped capability in knee imaging.

**5.4.16 Knee Injection**

- The accuracy benefit is established and is an anatomical endpoint.
- A more accurate injection of an ineffective agent is a more accurate nothing.
- Guidance's value depends on the agent and the indication.
- The pre-scan's diagnostic contribution is rarely recorded.
- The imaging claim stops at placement.

**5.4.17 Aspiration**

- The planning scan also decides whether the procedure should happen.
- A Doppler finding is the only urgent change ultrasound makes here.
- Imaging cannot distinguish infected from inflammatory fluid.
- Guidance's value is inversely matched to its common use.
- Doppler first, laboratory for the fluid, magnetic resonance for the cause.

**5.4.18 Hydrodissection**

- The technique exists only under ultrasound, so its evidence base is young.
- Expertise and enthusiasm are concentrated in the same people.
- A satisfying visual endpoint is a technical endpoint.
- Durable symptom change in the knee is not established.
- The imaging makes the technique safe and does not make it indicated.

**5.4.19 PRP Procedures**

- The imaging is settled and the treatment's efficacy is contested.
- Protocols differ in several variables and only delivery location is an imaging one.
- The appearance being treated occurs in asymptomatic tendons.
- The pre-procedure scan may contribute most and is rarely recorded.
- Reproducible delivery is a precondition for answering efficacy, not an answer.

**5.4.20 Tendon Procedures**

- Imaging, needle and indication belong to one person here.
- That is the source of the usefulness and of the weak accountability.
- The selection step is weaker than the targeting step.
- The outcome literature mixes procedures imaging cannot distinguish.
- Each of ultrasound's strengths is recorded in a form that does not survive.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.4.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
