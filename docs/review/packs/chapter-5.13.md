## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.13.1 Bone Marrow Oedema**

- History, alignment and an interval beat another sequence.
- Serial comparison across scanners is partly technical.
- Metabolic imaging reports activity and is rarely available.
- Automation shares the reader's differential problem.
- Report the pattern and the differential rather than a name.

**5.13.2 Stress Reactions**

- Grades from different systems are not comparable.
- A return to play led by the scan is later than needed.
- Recurrence at one site points away from the bone.
- Multiple sites take the problem out of the knee.
- The report should state what it cannot say about cause.

**5.13.3 Stress Fractures**

- The answer to failed healing is rarely in the images.
- A line with no oedema around it is a non-union.
- A site that does not fit the activity is pathological until proven.
- Repeat scanning measures protocols alongside healing.
- The report should state the site's known behaviour.

**5.13.4 Insufficiency Fractures**

- An arthroscopy treating the meniscus alone often worsens it.
- Imaging after surgery cannot separate course from effect.
- The old name persists and has to be corrected.
- Bone quality is not a radiological finding.
- Serial imaging needs the same projection every time.

**5.13.5 Osteonecrosis**

- The cause is often unexamined once the lesion is named.
- Steroid exposure is historical and absent from referrals.
- The plate is best watched radiographically.
- A treated lesion has no agreed expected appearance.
- The report should leave the systemic question open.

**5.13.6 Transient Osteoporosis**

- Two scans from two departments rarely compare.
- The history across joints is the most informative thing.
- A fracture during the interval is expected rather than surprising.
- Advanced sequences do not shorten the interval.
- The report should name the protocol the comparison needs.

**5.13.7 Bone Tumours**

- These present as a knee treated for something else.
- A previous procedure changes the resection and the prognosis.
- Prior imaging records the untouched anatomy.
- The history outweighs reassuring imaging features.
- Recognition and referral are the job.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.13.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
