## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**2.3.1 Anatomy**

- Find the nerve rather than estimate it; it is identified behind the biceps tendon and followed round the neck.
- The fibula is not a fixed point, which is an argument for tibial based posterolateral reconstruction.
- Any procedure at the fibular head is a nerve operation as much as a bone operation.
- The superior tibiofibular joint constrains correction in high tibial osteotomy.

**2.3.2 Attachments**

- The fibular attachments are the uncontested part of a contested region.
- Varus restraint and rotational restraint are separate goals with separate anatomy.
- The collateral and popliteofibular ligaments leave nearly the same point in different directions.
- A finding that differs from the textbook here is more often variation than injury.

**2.3.3 Clinical Importance**

- The biceps tendon is the reliable landmark when the field is scarred or the knee dislocated.
- Compression localises to the tunnel; traction damages the nerve over a length, and the prognoses differ.
- The nerve is fixed proximally behind the biceps and distally in the tunnel, so the mobile segment is short.
- A nerve deficit in a dislocated knee changes the order of reconstruction and the counselling.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-2.3.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
