## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.11.1 Iliotibial Band Function**

- The tissue a tenodesis uses and the structure it reproduces are different statements.
- The restraint depends on the femoral fixation rather than on the tissue used.
- Routing deep to the lateral collateral sets the line of action.
- A strip harvest does not produce a measurable varus deficit in reported series.
- Femoral fixation is what makes a tenodesis a joint restraint.

**3.11.2 Kaplan Fibre Function**

- The distinction changes the fixation point a little and the rationale a lot.
- Cruciate tunnel, tenodesis fixation and corner reconstruction compete for the distal lateral femur.
- Knowing the footprints is how convergence is avoided.
- Route deep to the collateral and fix proximal and posterior to its origin.
- The optimal point within the region is not established.

**3.11.3 Anterolateral Ligament Function**

- The variables are over-constraint risk, tunnel competition and graft quantity.
- An anatomic reconstruction adds a tibial tunnel; a tenodesis does not.
- That is a geometric argument favouring the tenodesis in a crowded knee.
- It is independent of the evidence argument.
- No measurement establishes which construct suits a given knee.

**3.11.4 Pivot Shift Control**

- Pivot shift control is the outcome a lateral procedure is added to achieve.
- The grade is the indication.
- The cruciate graft restores its share; the anterolateral share is not restored by it.
- Residual shift is associated with poorer outcome and graft failure.
- No threshold defines an acceptable residual shift.

**3.11.5 Internal Rotation Control**

- Internal rotation control is what the anterolateral debate is about.
- Over-constraint is the cost that makes it a real debate.
- Excessive tension or external rotation at fixation restricts internal rotation.
- Trials have not shown an excess of stiffness or lateral compartment problems.
- No clinical measurement detects over-constraint before it is symptomatic.

**3.11.6 Segond Lesion Mechanics**

- The lesion is direct evidence of anterolateral injury, usually only inferred.
- The fragment is not fixed unless it is large and displaced.
- Its value is diagnostic and prognostic rather than therapeutic.
- That is an unusual position for a fracture and worth stating.
- No evidence establishes that fixing it changes the result.

**3.11.7 Lateral Extra-articular Tenodesis**

- The indications are a list rather than a principle.
- Each item is a statement about rotational demand or restraint.
- That is the principle the list is a proxy for.
- Femoral fixation crowding and cruciate tunnel interaction are the practical constraints.
- No measurement identifies the individual who needs one.

**3.11.8 Combined ACL and ALL Reconstruction**

- The revision knee may need graft, lateral construct and slope correction together.
- A slope correcting osteotomy changes the anatomy a cruciate tunnel will use.
- A tenodesis adds no tibial tunnel, which is a geometric advantage in revision.
- Sequencing and tunnel geometry decide what is possible in one sitting.
- No measurement establishes the optimal sequence.

**3.11.9 Contemporary Controversies**

- Augmentation adds restraint; slope correction reduces demand.
- That is the difference between a stronger rope and a lighter load.
- They are not equivalent and no trial compares them directly.
- Previous failure raises the threshold for doing less.
- Both may be needed in the knee that failed once.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-3.11.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
