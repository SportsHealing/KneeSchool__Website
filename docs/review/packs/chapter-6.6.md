**6.6.1 Epidemiology**

- Families hear a risk statement as a verdict on the sport.
- The risk is a reason to train differently rather than to withdraw.
- The benefits of the sport are not in the calculation unless stated.
- Demand in this group is set by a season, a squad and a coach.
- Ask who decides when she plays.

**6.6.2 Hormonal Factors**

- The honest answer sounds evasive and a confident wrong answer is rewarded.
- Pair the declined factor with the one that can guide decisions.
- Low energy availability belongs in the conversation on its own merits.
- A cause located in her body is heard differently from one in her training.
- Replace the explanation she arrived with rather than denying it.

**6.6.3 Neuromuscular Factors**

- The content of this page is delivered by other people.
- What is available differs from what is indicated.
- Availability varies by squad, school and postcode.
- An athlete told she moves wrongly may stop moving freely.
- Ask who will coach the landing.

**6.6.4 Prevention**

- The most effective thing here sits outside the consultation.
- Describing a programme is not treatment and is usually left undone.
- A clinic measured by operations does not count this work.
- An athlete who believes nothing can be done will do nothing.
- Tell her what exists.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-6.6.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
