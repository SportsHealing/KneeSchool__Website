## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.6.1 Functions of the Meniscus**

- The five functions cannot be restored selectively.
- A repair restores all five to some degree; a device that restores area restores one.
- No current substitute restores function to the level of a repaired native meniscus.
- The decision taken at the first operation is the one that matters.
- Nothing measures restored meniscal function in a patient.

**3.6.2 Hoop Stress**

- Hoop tension is the quantity every meniscal operation is judged against.
- Repairability is a question about whether tension can be restored.
- A repair can heal without restoring the fibre path.
- Cyclic gap formation is the construct losing the tension path.
- No measurement confirms restored hoop tension in a patient.

**3.6.3 Circumferential Fibres**

- A vertical suture captures the circumferential bundles; a horizontal one lies between them.
- The vertical mattress advantage is architectural rather than a preference.
- A construct can be well oriented and fail if the bundles no longer carry tension.
- Failure mode in a construct study is as informative as failure load.
- Histology shows fibre path reconstitution; clinical imaging cannot.

**3.6.4 Radial Fibres**

- A longitudinal tear needs cohesion restored; a radial tear needs the hoop path restored.
- A construct chosen for one pattern is not automatically right for the other.
- Radial repair is harder because the construct carries the hoop tension until healing.
- Cyclic gap formation matters more for a radial repair.
- Healing rates should be read by tear pattern, not in aggregate.

**3.6.5 Load Sharing**

- A small amount of tissue carries 85 per cent of a compartment's load at 90 degrees.
- Posterior root repair is a load path operation, not a tidying one.
- Restoring area in extension is easier than restoring the flexion share.
- A transplant participates in flexion only if its horns are fixed.
- The deepest part of the range is the least characterised.

**3.6.6 Shock Absorption**

- Damping has the weakest quantitative evidence of the five functions and a sound mechanism.
- It supports activity advice rather than a treatment argument.
- Allograft and scaffolds do not reproduce native damping properties.
- No clinical measurement of a patient's meniscal damping exists.
- Build the preservation argument on load distribution instead.

**3.6.7 Joint Lubrication**

- No operation targets lubrication and no measurement would confirm an effect.
- Its value is explanatory: why raised stress degenerates faster than stress alone predicts.
- Injectables target lubrication molecularly, not meniscally.
- Tribological testing in vitro is the only direct measurement.
- No measurement links a patient's meniscal status to their joint's lubrication.

**3.6.8 Proprioception**

- The sensory argument coincides with the load bearing one for preserving horns.
- A transplant restores tissue, not innervation; reinnervation is partial.
- Proprioceptive recovery after transplantation is incomplete.
- No measurement attributes a deficit specifically to lost meniscal proprioception.
- Do not rest a treatment argument on proprioception alone.

**3.6.9 Root Biomechanics**

- A strong but misplaced root repair restores an anchor in the wrong direction.
- Non anatomic repair restores contact mechanics less well, by conventional teaching.
- Position decides the mechanics more than fixation strength does.
- The anterior medial root and the cruciate tunnel compete for the same bone.
- No clinical measurement confirms a repaired root carries hoop tension.

**3.6.10 Meniscal Extrusion**

- Extrusion is the closest available proxy for whether a meniscus is working.
- Persisting extrusion after root repair indicates the hoop was not restored.
- A transplant that extrudes is not in the load path.
- An uncorrected malalignment will push a restored meniscus out again.
- No measurement of meniscal function itself exists, which is why extrusion carries weight.

**3.6.11 Effects of Root Tears**

- Root repair is a joint preservation operation rather than a symptom operation.
- The footprint centre must be restored to restore the direction of tension.
- A repaired root in an uncorrected varus limb carries the load that detached the original.
- Cyclic displacement is the construct losing the anchor position.
- Residual extrusion is the available proxy for restored function.

**3.6.12 Effects of Radial Tears**

- The radial tear has the most mechanical consequence and the least reliable healing.
- Lower healing rates follow from the construct carrying the hoop tension.
- Cyclic gap formation matters more than ultimate strength for this pattern.
- Load protection after repair is a mechanical requirement.
- No measurement confirms the tension path was restored.

**3.6.13 Meniscal Repair Biomechanics**

- The difficult practice is where the three requirements cannot all be met.
- Mechanics say what would be restored, not whether restoring it is worth the operation.
- A root repair with a correcting osteotomy is more than additive.
- Cartilage state sets the ceiling on what preservation achieves.
- No measurement predicts which individual knee will preserve.

**3.6.14 Meniscal Transplantation Biomechanics**

- Transplantation is rarely a single procedure and the order has mechanical consequences.
- A graft in an uncorrected environment is loaded by what destroyed its predecessor.
- Scaffolds restore area without reproducing the tensile architecture.
- Combined procedure series are small and heterogeneous.
- No measurement separates each component's contribution in a combined procedure.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-3.6.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
