## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**6.8.1 Graft Failure**

- Assigning a cause means judging a colleague's operation in a letter.
- Differs from current practice is usually the accurate statement.
- Demand caused the first failure in a proportion of cases.
- A second failure is received very differently from a first.
- Say what the second operation cannot restore before choosing it.

**6.8.2 Tunnel Malposition**

- This is the revision finding with a professional consequence attached.
- Describe position, state the standard, and avoid the word error.
- A staged revision costs a year of the patient's life.
- Whether a rotational deficiency matters depends on demand.
- Separate the measurement from the attribution when saying it aloud.

**6.8.3 Biological Failure**

- This is the explanation that blames nobody.
- The incentives point one way and the evidence does not.
- Smoking is the modifiable factor with a plausible mechanism.
- A patient told their biology failed hears something about themselves.
- Answer the question about stopping honestly rather than deferring it.

**6.8.4 Infection**

- Uncommon enough to be unfamiliar and urgent enough that unfamiliarity costs.
- Set the aspiration threshold low in advance, as a standing position.
- Comorbidities change both the risk and the presentation.
- A patient reassured once is slower to return a second time.
- Make the aspiration threshold a policy rather than a decision.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-6.8.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
