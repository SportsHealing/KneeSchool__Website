## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.11.1 Normal Cartilage**

- Morphology reports shape, and mechanical state determines survival.
- Compositional values do not transfer between sequences and field strengths.
- Early matrix change precedes any morphological change.
- The period intervention might alter is the period the routine study cannot see.
- The shape reported is not the shape the joint uses.

**5.11.2 Chondral Defects**

- Imaging underestimates depth and area against inspection, consistently.
- The imaging is a screening step rather than a planning one.
- A grade without its protocol is not comparable with a threshold.
- The matrix state of the surrounding cartilage is not routinely assessed.
- Report the four variables and call depth and area minimum estimates.

**5.11.3 Fissuring**

- Reported prevalence depends on the protocol more than on the population.
- The clinically interesting window precedes the visible one.
- The significance of isolated fissuring is not established.
- Improved sensitivity arrives with a changed reference.
- Say that the finding is at the resolution limit.

**5.11.4 Delamination**

- For this lesion the reference standard is the weaker test.
- Published accuracy figures for it are structurally unreliable.
- The anatomy that makes imaging superior also makes it uncertain.
- Prevalence is probably underestimated.
- Look at the deep interface where marrow change accompanies an intact surface.

**5.11.5 Osteochondral Lesions**

- The fluid sign also appears with granulation or a healing interface.
- A weakly specific sign carries the decision because the alternative is invasive.
- Bony depth in a sclerotic bed is understated here.
- A complete assessment needs two studies and usually gets one.
- Viability is inferred from morphology.

**5.11.6 OCD**

- The interface fluid sign supports opposite conclusions.
- Staging systems were derived on particular sequences.
- A stage without its protocol is not comparable.
- A knee protocol records skeletal maturity only incidentally.
- Report the four observations and let the stage follow from them.

**5.11.7 Bone Marrow Lesions**

- Population level associations are quoted as individual predictions.
- Prevalence in asymptomatic knees means it shifts probability very little.
- Quantitative volume measurement is a research technique.
- A qualitative finding is used quantitatively.
- The patterned acute injury is its most reliable and least discussed use.

**5.11.8 Cartilage Repair Procedures**

- Three Section 5 chapters reach the same conclusion about repair.
- The measurable surrogate is morphological in each case.
- The weak surrogate relationship suggests the modality rather than the tissue.
- Compositional values do not transfer between sequences and field strengths.
- Imaging documents a cartilage repair and does not evaluate it.

**5.11.9 ACI Imaging**

- A long morphological series accumulates cost without predictive power.
- The technique best suited to the question is the least available.
- The scoring systems relate weakly to patient reported outcome.
- The procedure has largely been superseded by its successor.
- Image on the schedule the decision needs, and report the trend.

**5.11.10 MACI Imaging**

- The membrane introduced a deep interface failure mode.
- Inherited templates focus on fill and surface.
- The appearance is the same as native delamination.
- Carried over scores do not include attachment.
- Add the deep interface to the report as a named observation.

**5.11.11 Osteochondral Grafts**

- This procedure's endpoint is bony union, which is directly visible.
- That makes its follow up more useful than for cell based procedures.
- The advantage comes from the surgery rather than from better imaging.
- The part imaging assesses well is the part that fails least often.
- Allograft adds a viability question morphology does not answer.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.11.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
