## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.16.1 Fracture Assessment**

- A scan after fixation cannot recover the original anatomy.
- Classification systems were calibrated on lower resolution.
- Automated detection is weakest on the impaction.
- Repeat scanning accumulates dose in young patients.
- The report should state the soft tissue question as open.

**5.16.2 Tibial Plateau Fractures**

- Metal artefact sits over the surface the revision concerns.
- The original scan is the only pre-operative record.
- Follow-up for arthritis is radiographic.
- Classification agreement is a weakness a better scan does not fix.
- The report should state the soft tissue question as open.

**5.16.3 Rotational Profiles**

- An unnamed axis makes a value unusable.
- Remeasuring needs the images and not the report.
- A correction invalidates every later comparison.
- Deformity reported one plane at a time is understated.
- Automated consistency is not validity.

**5.16.4 TT-TG Measurement**

- A value in a letter usually arrives with no method.
- Remeasuring needs the images.
- Alternative measurements disagree legitimately.
- After a transfer the measurement describes the operation.
- The method belongs before the value in the report.

**5.16.5 Osteotomy Planning**

- A previous correction moves the reference points.
- The planes interact and are planned separately.
- A film cannot show whether the ligaments hold the correction.
- Software removes variability and hides landmark uncertainty.
- The report should state the receiving compartment.

**5.16.6 Arthroplasty Planning**

- Artefact is worst at the interface the decision rests on.
- A scan reclassifies radiographic bone loss grades.
- Serial imaging across departments compares protocols.
- Automated segmentation carries one error into every case.
- The report should name the rotational reference used.

**5.16.7 Robotic Surgery Planning**

- The reviewer did not acquire the scan or check the segmentation.
- A plan is not portable between systems.
- The model is least reliable where it is most relied on.
- The planning dose recurs across the pathway.
- The precision is geometric and the judgement is functional.

**5.16.8 3D Reconstructions**

- A chosen orientation hides whatever faces away.
- A printed model presents an interpretation as an object.
- Renderings from different presets are not comparable.
- Automated pipelines choose the threshold silently.
- A rendering is a figure in an argument.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.16.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
