## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**4.22.1 IKDC**

- An instrument is validated for a population and a purpose.
- Two patients with the same score can have different problems.
- Importance thresholds are published quantities not stated here.
- Expectation shapes the score as much as the knee does.
- Ceiling and floor effects truncate the hardest populations.

**4.22.2 KOOS**

- Five subscales mean five comparisons, which needs interpretive care.
- The domains are not independent of each other.
- Each subscale has its own ceiling and floor.
- Importance thresholds differ by subscale and are not stated here.
- A profile describes a state without explaining it.

**4.22.3 Lysholm**

- Its items reflect the activities and expectations of its period.
- It is not a pure patient reported measure despite its use as one.
- Ceiling effects are more marked than in instruments with a sport domain.
- Comparability argues for collecting it alongside a modern instrument.
- No claim about its psychometric properties is made here.

**4.22.4 Tegner**

- One ordered list compresses sport, frequency, intensity and position.
- The pre injury level is recalled and recall is systematically generous.
- The levels reflect the sports of a particular time and place.
- The same level is not the same performance.
- Activity level is a confounder in every comparison that omits it.

**4.22.5 Oxford Knee Score**

- The ceiling compresses the difference between good and excellent.
- A patient dissatisfied about sport or kneeling is poorly described by a good score.
- A difference between services can reflect populations rather than surgery.
- A single total discards the pattern.
- Importance thresholds are not stated here.

**4.22.6 ACL-RSI**

- The score is an intervention target rather than a trait.
- Fear and capacity are entangled, so the battery's separation is incomplete.
- A score completed for the decider is subject to an obvious incentive.
- Its relationship to return and reinjury is a literature question.
- Acting on the finding may be a referral question.

**4.22.7 Sportshealing Knee Score**

- The development stages are nameable and none is in this repository.
- The seven domains overlap, so an unreduced item set would double count.
- Spanning two populations doubles the validation work.
- A site specific instrument needs published development work as its defence.
- The honest description is seven domains and no instrument.

**4.22.8 PROMs in Clinical Practice**

- A mean score across a service describes nobody.
- Importance thresholds differ by instrument and population and none is stated here.
- Different expectations make a between population comparison not about treatment.
- Missing data in a collected series is not missing at random.
- An instrument applied outside its population produces numbers of unknown meaning.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-4.22.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
