## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.4.1 Introduction to Kinetics**

- Cartilage responds to load, so the kinetic account explains progression.
- The knee adduction moment is the actionable kinetic quantity because it is modifiable.
- Most knee operations are kinetic interventions described anatomically.
- Musculoskeletal models resolve the redundancy inverse dynamics leaves, by assumption.
- No routine clinical measurement of joint load exists.

**3.4.2 Ground Reaction Forces**

- Alignment surgery changes where the force line falls, not the force.
- Gait retraining and a lateral wedge move the line; their effect sizes are small.
- The external knee adduction moment is the derived quantity that matters.
- Static alignment and the dynamic moment correlate imperfectly.
- No clinic measurement gives the adduction moment, so alignment stands in for it.

**3.4.3 Joint Reaction Forces**

- Compartment load division is what osteotomy and unicompartmental surgery manipulate.
- Lateral overload after overcorrection is the kinetic account of a technical error.
- The 60 to 40 division is an average over a cycle, not an instantaneous truth.
- Instrumented telemetry validates the models that estimate intact knee loads.
- Intraoperative pressure sensing describes an anaesthetised knee.

**3.4.4 Lever Arms**

- The extensor lever arm is a surgical variable in joint line, patella and tubercle surgery.
- Joint line elevation shortens the extensor arm and raises patellofemoral load.
- Anteromedialisation is an explicit lever arm and load distribution operation.
- The arm is managed through proxies, since no intraoperative measurement exists.
- Patellar height should be measured before and after extensor mechanism surgery.

**3.4.5 Moments About the Knee**

- The adduction moment is modifiable by osteotomy, gait modification and bracing.
- The sagittal moment sets the extensor demand and the rehabilitation target.
- A moment is the quantity in which unlike treatments can be compared.
- The link between the moment and progression is association, not a causal threshold.
- No moment threshold defines the osteotomy indication.

**3.4.6 External Moments**

- Alignment is the lever that moves the adduction moment furthest.
- Dose response: osteotomy large, bracing moderate, gait modification small.
- The association with progression is observational, with no randomised threshold.
- Body mass can exceed the surgical effect in magnitude.
- No agreed moment threshold defines the indication.

**3.4.7 Internal Moments**

- Surgery changes geometry and restraint; it does not supply a quadriceps.
- A graft restores a passive moment that acts at the end of range only.
- Functional instability with a tight graft is absent mid range muscular control.
- A hamstring graft removes part of the internal flexion moment.
- No clinical measurement separates the construct's contribution from the muscle's.

**3.4.8 Energy Absorption**

- A limb that cannot absorb avoids the task or passes energy to the joint surface.
- Absorption asymmetry persists after reconstruction behind a normal examination.
- Loading rate and cumulative cycles drive the cartilage consequence, not a single peak.
- Hop tests measure performance and have a ceiling effect.
- No measurement predicts an individual's tolerance of repeated absorption.

**3.4.9 Force Transmission**

- Every reconstructive decision is a load path decision.
- Correct alignment before or with a cartilage or meniscal procedure.
- Meniscal substitution restores area rather than tissue.
- Subchondral bone is the most often ignored part of the path.
- A cartilage procedure restores a surface of differing stiffness from the original.

**3.4.10 Shock Absorption**

- Activity advice follows from which absorbing structure has been lost.
- Impact sport is a rate problem; occupational kneeling is a cumulative one.
- Subchondral marrow lesions mark where absorption has failed.
- Muscle capacity is the absorbing contributor treatment can change.
- No measurement predicts which patient tolerates a given activity.
