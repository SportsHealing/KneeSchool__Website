## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.14.1 Synovitis**

- A diagnostic arthroscopy is rarely the right next step.
- Treated disease is partly a record of the treatment.
- Quantified synovial volume carries the usual problem.
- Automation inherits the differential.
- The report should state that infection is not excluded.

**5.14.2 Rheumatoid Arthritis**

- The joint is shaped by decades of treatment.
- A textbook comparison is the wrong reference.
- Bone stock decides the operation and is hard to judge near metal.
- Infection carries a higher prior probability here.
- Serial radiographs remain the best record of progression.

**5.14.3 Psoriatic Arthritis**

- Several or bilateral attachments should prompt a referral.
- A procedure into an inflammatory enthesis is the harm to avoid.
- Treated disease differs from progression.
- Imaging cannot separate the spondyloarthropathies.
- The pattern is suggestive rather than diagnostic.

**5.14.4 Crystal Arthropathy**

- The imaging was right and the inference was wrong.
- Deposition accelerates after meniscectomy and arthroplasty.
- Dual energy computed tomography answers what the aspirate does.
- An attack in a replaced knee still needs an aspirate.
- The report should direct an acute knee to aspiration.

**5.14.5 PVNS/TGCT**

- Scar and residual tumour share the signal.
- The first post-operative scan is a baseline.
- The pre-operative extent is the valuable record.
- Cartilage loss changes whether excision is right.
- The report should name the posterior compartment.

**5.14.6 Synovial Chondromatosis**

- Retained and new bodies cannot be told apart.
- The pre-operative map answers what imaging cannot.
- Recurrence with cartilage loss is a different problem.
- A change in character matters more than one in number.
- Serial imaging needs a single protocol.

**5.14.7 Septic Arthritis**

- Partial treatment makes everything harder to read.
- Artefact sits at the interface the question concerns.
- Metabolic imaging cannot separate infection from inflammation.
- Imaging after treatment lags the clinical course.
- Imaging supports management and does not diagnose.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.14.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
