## Claims to confirm

**0.1.1 Meet Your Knee**

- The knee is where the thigh bone and the shin bone meet, with the kneecap at the front.
- The fibula sits on the outside and carries very little weight.
- Ligaments, menisci, cartilage, muscles and a slippery lining fill the spaces.
- The bones alone would slide apart, so the soft parts provide the stability.
- The knee moves a long way, carries a lot, and stays steady, all at once.

**0.1.2 Bones, Ligaments, Menisci and Muscles**

- Four groups: bones, ligaments, menisci and muscles with their tendons.
- Ligaments join bone to bone. Tendons join muscle to bone.
- The two cruciate ligaments sit inside the joint and cross each other.
- The menisci are two C shaped pads between the thigh bone and the shin bone.
- Each group fails in its own way, which is why naming the part matters.

**0.1.3 How the Knee Moves**

- The knee is more than a hinge: it rolls and glides at the same time.
- Rolling alone would carry the thigh bone off the back of the shin bone.
- The knee also twists a few degrees, most when bent and least when straight.
- At full straightening the joint locks, so standing costs little muscle effort.
- Twisting on a planted foot is how many sports injuries happen.

**0.1.4 The Knee Under Load**

- Walking on the flat sends roughly three times your body weight through each knee.
- Stairs, squatting and landing send considerably more.
- The menisci and cartilage spread force over a wide area, which lowers pressure.
- Bending as you land spreads the force over a longer moment.
- Strong muscles do that bending, which is why strong legs protect knees.

**0.1.5 Growing Knees**

- Growth plates are discs of cartilage near the ends of growing bones.
- The two at the knee produce about two thirds of the length of your leg.
- They close in the mid to late teens, generally earlier in girls than boys.
- While open, the growth plate is weaker than the ligaments around it.
- Growth plate cartilage does not show on an x-ray, so tenderness is taken seriously.

**0.1.6 Curriculum Links**

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-0.1.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
