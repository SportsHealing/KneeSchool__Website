## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**2.11.1 Iliotibial Band**

- The band is anchored to the femur along a length, through the intermuscular septum.
- Its deep layers and the anterolateral ligament occupy the same region.
- A lateral release crosses the band's anterior expansion, so the procedures overlap.
- It is the main passive restraint to internal rotation at the knee.
- Over-tensioning removes rotation rather than restraining it.

**2.11.2 Kaplan Fibres**

- Three names share one function: Kaplan fibres, capsulo-osseous layer, anterolateral ligament.
- A lateral extra-articular procedure reproduces this attachment rather than adding a structure.
- Short fibres with little compliance make over-constraint a real risk.
- Division is routine in several standard exposures and almost never recorded.
- In a revision with a persistent pivot shift, this field is examined rather than assumed.

**2.11.3 Anterolateral Ligament**

- The argument is about naming; three structures share the function and the dispute changes no operation.
- Reconstruction targets a function, because the femoral footprint is not reproducible.
- Over-constraint is noticed by patients in a way a slight pivot shift is not.
- Indication is the live question; the anatomy says only that there is something to reconstruct.
- A Segond fragment is the one piece of direct evidence of the attachment a surgeon sees.

**2.11.4 Segond Lesion**

- It is the only direct observation of the anterolateral attachment in a living patient.
- Its donor site and an anterolateral tibial fixation occupy the same cortex.
- It is a rotational injury marker rather than a fracture needing treatment.
- Its presence has been associated with higher grade rotatory laxity.
- Record it even when nothing is done to it.

**2.11.5 Anterolateral Complex**

- A century of naming disagreement has not changed the operation, because it targets a region.
- A fellowship position here is a position on the evidence, not on the dissection.
- Over-constraint presents as a knee that does not feel like the patient's own.
- A divided complex is the commonest explanation for a pivot shift after a sound graft.
- A Segond fragment is the only direct evidence of the attachment outside a dissection room.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-2.11.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
