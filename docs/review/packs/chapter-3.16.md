## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.16.1 Cadaveric Testing**

- Each cut changes the knee the next cut is made in.
- Randomising the order addresses it and halves the usable data, so many studies do not.
- Specimen numbers are small: a precise measurement of a few knees, not a population estimate.
- Between specimen variation often exceeds the effect, which is why paired designs are used.
- Reported precision is the rig's, not the biology's.

**3.16.2 Robotic Testing Systems**

- Superposition assumes the repeated path behaves the same, which preconditioning addresses.
- Precision is excellent and accuracy relative to a living knee is unknown.
- Simulated muscle loads change contributions for muscle associated structures.
- The simulated pivot shift converted a clinical sign into a repeatable input.
- No study has validated robotic kinematics against the same knees in vivo.

**3.16.3 Navigation Systems**

- Navigation improves precision of hitting a target and says nothing about the target.
- Outliers fall, mean alignment improves, patient reported outcomes move very little.
- Its second use is as a measurement tool generating otherwise unobtainable data.
- That data supported the kinematic alignment argument.
- Accuracy studies compare two measurements, so neither is ground truth.

**3.16.4 Finite Element Modelling**

- A model can put the same knee in two conditions, which no experiment can.
- Every absolute number carries assumptions that are rarely varied to test their weight.
- Sensitivity analysis is the honest response and appears in a minority of papers.
- Subject specific geometry with generic material properties is partial specificity.
- Internal stress has never been validated directly, because there is nothing to validate against.

**3.16.5 Computational Biomechanics**

- Electromyography driven models replace the optimisation assumption with measurement.
- That matters most in the arthritic knee, where co-contraction is raised.
- Blind prediction competitions found no single model accurate across subjects and activities.
- Scaled generic models carry the original specimen's muscle geometry.
- The native knee's contact forces remain unvalidated, because validation comes from implants.

**3.16.6 Artificial Intelligence**

- The dataset is the model, and it is the part least often published.
- Knee datasets come from populations selected by reaching imaging and surgery.
- External validation is the test that matters; cross validation is reported in its place.
- A model trained on radiologist labels learns radiologists.
- Explainability usually describes what was attended to rather than why.

**3.16.7 Motion Capture**

- Reliability is good sagittally, acceptable coronally and poor in the transverse plane.
- The contested rotational questions sit in the plane this method measures worst.
- Biplanar fluoroscopy tracks bone directly at a radiation cost and over a few paces.
- Markerless capture removes placement variation and not soft tissue artefact.
- Artefact is estimated and sometimes modelled, not corrected.

**3.16.8 Wearable Technology**

- The transformative possibility is continuous measurement rather than better measurement.
- A wearable samples the behaviour that produced the problem.
- Validation is uneven: steps well, angles moderately, loads barely.
- Sparse sensor data becomes a joint estimate through machine learning.
- Validating a field device in a laboratory is the field's standing awkwardness.

**3.16.9 Digital Twins**

- The plausible version is narrow: one tissue, one data stream, one question.
- The hard part is estimating this person's material properties without a biopsy.
- Without them, a twin is a population model wearing one patient's shape.
- Validating an individual prediction needs prospective follow up of that individual.
- No knee twin has been prospectively validated to predict an individual's outcome.

**3.16.10 Future Directions**

- Measurement capability has outrun validation for twenty years and the gap is widening.
- Producing a new method is publishable and validating an old one is not.
- The named gaps are robotic external validity, internal stress, native contact force and wearable thresholds.
- None is impossible; each is unfunded relative to the next novel method.
- There is no accepted metric for how well validated a field is.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-3.16.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
