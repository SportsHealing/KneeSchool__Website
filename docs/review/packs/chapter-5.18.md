## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.18.1 Quantitative MRI**

- Validation in a small series is presented as validation.
- Group differences are not within patient sensitivity.
- A native reference range does not apply to repaired tissue.
- Automation removes operator variability and not scanner variability.
- Standardisation rather than discovery is the obstacle.

**5.18.2 T2 Mapping**

- Harmonisation across sites is itself a study result.
- Two scanners make an uncomparable pair.
- An abnormal map in a comfortable knee is common.
- Automation inherits the deep zone edge problem.
- It is an outcome measure rather than a diagnostic test.

**5.18.3 T1 Rho Imaging**

- Histological validation exists only in small series.
- Implementations differ between sites under one name.
- The measure's basis does not transfer to repair tissue.
- Combining maps multiplies the standardisation problem.
- The most promising measure is the furthest from use.

**5.18.4 UTE Imaging**

- The literature is thin and the claims are confident.
- Normal variation has not been mapped.
- Combining techniques compounds both problems.
- Automation is applied before readers have a normal.
- The newest technique has the least ground under it.

**5.18.5 Weight-Bearing MRI**

- The request usually seeks a finding already believed in.
- Findings are device specific.
- Lower field strength confounds load with image quality.
- The published literature favours positive studies.
- The right question, and almost nobody can ask it.

**5.18.6 Dynamic MRI**

- The study is asked to settle what it cannot.
- Implementations are not comparable.
- Guarding during the acquisition produces a normal study.
- The examination is cheaper and already available.
- Automation is built on unrepresentative movements.

**5.18.7 PET-MRI**

- The tracer determines the question.
- Normal uptake after surgery is not established.
- A confirmatory study does not justify its dose.
- Quantification carries the same standardisation problem.
- Most surgeons meet this as a report rather than a request.

**5.18.8 Artificial Intelligence in Imaging**

- Automated output arrives unmarked inside reports.
- Tertiary cases are under-represented in training data.
- A system update makes a serial comparison meaningless.
- An automated measurement looks settled and is not.
- Workflow will change before accuracy does.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.18.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
