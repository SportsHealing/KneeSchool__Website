## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.14.1 Normal Gait**

- Gait is the only measurement of the knee doing its actual job under its actual loads.
- It is almost never available to the clinician who would use it.
- Its strength is detecting adaptation the patient cannot report.
- Kinetic outputs are modelled, with an error chain through markers, segments and inverse dynamics.
- No method measures contact force in a native knee.

**3.14.2 Stance Phase**

- The stance flexion wave is the most informative single feature of gait at the knee.
- Pain, effusion, weakness and arthrofibrosis all flatten it and look alike.
- Losing it raises peak loading rate, because the compliant phase is gone.
- The adduction moment's first peak is the strongest reported link to medial progression.
- Named limps and measurable subphase parameters map onto each other poorly.

**3.14.3 Swing Phase**

- Swing is where a stiff knee declares itself and where clinic assessment rarely looks.
- Peak swing flexion is a sensitive marker of early stiffness.
- Rectus femoris dysfunction gives a stiff knee gait with normal passive range.
- Swing flexion recovers less completely than stance after arthroplasty.
- No clinical measurement separates a range problem from a timing problem.

**3.14.4 Running Gait**

- Running is where the gap between tissue capacity and applied load is narrowest.
- Cumulative load is the variable that matters and the one never measured.
- Cadence manipulation is the best evidenced gait retraining at the knee.
- Foot strike retraining moves load to the ankle rather than removing it.
- Teaching has moved from prescribing a technique to prescribing a load.

**3.14.5 Sprinting**

- Sprinting is the highest force gait here and the knee is not usually the structure at risk.
- Low stance flexion keeps quadriceps and patellofemoral demand from scaling with speed.
- The demand moves to the two joint muscles, particularly the hamstring in late swing.
- Linear sprinting is low risk for the cruciate; changing direction is not.
- Grouping running and sprinting in return to sport criteria is questionable.

**3.14.6 Cutting Manoeuvres**

- The injury mechanism was reconstructed from video and modelling, not measured.
- The 50 millisecond window means protection is whatever was already active at contact.
- That is the mechanical case for neuromuscular training.
- Prevention programmes reduce cruciate injury rates and depend entirely on adherence.
- Fear of reinjury changes cutting mechanics measurably.

**3.14.7 Jumping**

- Jump testing is the most widely used functional measurement and one of the least standardised.
- The 90 per cent symmetry threshold is a convention rather than a validated cutoff.
- Symmetry can be achieved by both limbs being poor.
- Quadriceps strength underlies the hop and is less confounded than it is.
- No single test predicts reinjury well, which is why batteries exist.

**3.14.8 Landing**

- Landing is the most screened movement in knee practice and the screening underperforms.
- Frontal plane measures predicting injury have been inconsistent.
- The intervention has held up better than the screen.
- The evidence supports training everybody more than selecting whom to train.
- The measurement is taken in a condition unlike the one in which injuries happen.

**3.14.9 ACL Deficient Gait**

- Quadriceps avoidance is the best known gait finding here and not the commonest.
- Reduced excursion, altered rotation and quadriceps deficit survive reconstruction in many.
- Persistent altered kinematics is the mechanical candidate for post traumatic degeneration.
- That reframes the aim from restoring laxity to restoring kinematics.
- Follow up measures laxity and outcome scores, not the variables gait says matter.

**3.14.10 PCL Deficient Gait**

- This is the clearest example of an adaptation that works and bills later.
- Quadriceps substitution restores function and produces the loading that follows.
- That argues for rehabilitation as treatment and implicates it in the long term cost.
- No trial compares long term joint outcomes between reconstruction and rehabilitation.
- The causal chain is plausible, consistent and not prospectively demonstrated.

**3.14.11 Arthritic Gait**

- Gait analysis has its strongest clinical claim here and the claim is still not acted on.
- The first peak adduction moment predicts progression better than the static angle.
- Interventions that lower it have measurable mechanical and modest symptomatic effects.
- Osteotomy lowers it by less than its degrees of correction imply.
- No clinical pathway measures the quantity that best predicts the outcome being prevented.

**3.14.12 Post-Arthroplasty Gait**

- A successful knee replacement does not restore normal knee mechanics.
- Reduced excursion and quadriceps deficit persist after operations everyone calls successful.
- Preoperative pattern predicts postoperative pattern strongly.
- Instrumented implants give the only direct in vivo knee load data, from a tiny sample.
- Patient reported scores have a ceiling that conceals what gait analysis finds.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-3.14.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
