## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**2.4.1 Anatomy**

- Every anterior exposure crosses the patellar vascular ring and every lateral release divides part of it.
- The mechanism responds to composite thickness, not to resection depth.
- Overstuffing limits flexion; understuffing leaves the mechanism slack and the bone at risk.
- Patellar bone stock is finite, so each procedure constrains the next.

**2.4.2 Ossification**

- Excising a superolateral fragment removes part of the vastus lateralis insertion and changes tracking.
- A bipartite patella is incidental far more often than symptomatic.
- Recognise a bipartite fragment before surgery or it will be mistaken for comminution.
- Both figures on this page vary between populations and are registered as unchecked.

**2.4.3 Biomechanics**

- Every patellofemoral procedure changes one of four relations, and that determines its failure mode.
- Load reduction and stability are separate aims that can conflict.
- Medialisation is the wrong direction in isolated medial facet disease, on geometric grounds.
- Arthroplasty changes the surface and tubercle surgery changes the geometry; the choice is which problem exists.

**2.4.4 Patellar Tracking**

- Establish which restraint failed; a flat trochlea and a torn ligament present identically to the patient.
- The first thirty degrees of flexion is the range that decides whether a reconstruction works.
- Stability and normal tracking are not the same thing, and the difference is a painful stable patella.
- Trochlear shape is the variable soft tissue surgery cannot change.

**2.4.5 Surgical Relevance**

- In the multiply operated patella the trochlea may be the only tracking variable left untouched.
- The mechanism survives without a patellar component but not without the bone.
- Patellar osteonecrosis is the complication whose anatomical and surgical causes are one sentence.
- Where the patella is unusable the reconstruction loses the moment arm permanently.
