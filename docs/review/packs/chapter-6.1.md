**6.1.1 Anterior cruciate ligament injury**

- The ACL runs from the posteromedial aspect of the lateral femoral condyle to the anterior intercondylar area of the tibia
- It carries anteromedial and posterolateral bundles named for their tibial insertions
- It is the primary restraint to anterior tibial translation and secondary restraint to internal rotation
- The classical mechanism is non contact deceleration or landing with valgus and internal rotation
- Female sex, previous injury, neuromuscular control and tibial slope are recognised risk contributors
- Haemarthrosis develops over the first few hours after rupture
- Lachman at twenty to thirty degrees is the most sensitive clinical test
- The anterior drawer is less sensitive acutely because of hamstring guarding
- A Segond fracture implies cruciate rupture until proven otherwise
- A tibial spine avulsion is the skeletally immature equivalent
- A deep lateral femoral notch sign is an indirect indicator
- MRI grades associated injury including ramp lesions of the posteromedial meniscocapsular junction
- Allograft in a young pivoting athlete carries a higher failure rate than autograft
- Arthrofibrosis risk is associated with operating on a stiff swollen knee with poor quadriceps control
- Listed failure modes: non anatomic tunnels, missed posterolateral corner injury, unaddressed ramp lesion or meniscal deficiency, increased posterior tibial slope, premature return to sport
- Lateral extra articular augmentation reduces graft failure in selected high risk patients
- Confluent, widened or badly malpositioned tunnels require staged revision with bone grafting
- Revision outcomes are poorer than primary outcomes for return to sport and patient reported scores
- Increased posterior tibial slope loads the graft
- Warm up programmes that train landing and turning lower ACL injury risk
- Girls and women tear the ACL more often than boys and men in the same sport
- A completely torn ACL does not heal back to original strength
- Repeated giving way episodes injure the meniscus and cartilage

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-6.1.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
