## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.7.1 Normal ACL**

- Normal ligament signal has no agreed definition.
- Knee position changes the line and tension and is not recorded.
- A structurally normal ligament can be functionally lax.
- The secondary signs carry the equivocal case.
- Automated reading reproduces the reader inconsistency it was trained on.

**5.7.2 Partial Tears**

- There is no agreed threshold of fibre loss for a partial tear.
- The published series on progression are not describing the same lesion.
- Fibre continuity and function diverge most in the middle range.
- Oblique sequences would help and are usually not acquired.
- The report starts a pathway the finding does not support.

**5.7.3 Complete Tears**

- The reconstruction decision is made on examination, not appearance.
- The associated injuries that matter most are detected worst.
- The level of the tear is often not described.
- Translation is measured unloaded and supine.
- The reporting convention has not followed the question.

**5.7.4 Acute ACL Injury**

- The findings repairable only acutely are detected worst acutely.
- Request timing matters more than the protocol here.
- The bone bruise is reported as carrying a prognosis it has not established.
- Supine translation and examined laxity are different quantities.
- A structured report would make the corners and roots mandatory fields.

**5.7.5 Chronic ACL Injury**

- The surrogates for a protectable compartment are supine and poorly standardised.
- A ligament protocol understates the cartilage loss that decides the case.
- Whether reconstruction alters the degenerative course is unsettled.
- Alignment and meniscal state sit in two different studies.
- Quantitative cartilage sequences would fit this decision and are not routine.

**5.7.6 Mucoid Degeneration**

- The appearance is common in asymptomatic knees.
- Whether it causes pain is unsettled and the surgical series are selected.
- Debridement removes an intact restraint and can produce laxity.
- The term spans a range with no threshold.
- Reporting calibre and continuity separately would remove most of the error.

**5.7.7 Ganglion Cysts**

- The pre-test probability that a given cyst explains a complaint is low.
- Whether this and mucoid change are one entity is unsettled.
- The treatment series are small, uncontrolled and selected on imaging.
- No size threshold for significance is established.
- Stating that a finding is probably incidental helps more than describing it well.

**5.7.8 ACL Healing**

- No imaging criterion for a ligament likely to heal is established.
- Continuity is used as an outcome and is least related to function.
- Stiffness is the quantity of interest and no clinical sequence measures it.
- Supine translation understates the laxity that matters.
- A report should say whether it supplied a structural or a functional measure.

**5.7.9 ACL Reconstruction**

- No graft signal to strength relationship is established well enough to act on.
- The study's most reliable content is its most variably reported.
- The failing question is rotational and the measurement is translational.
- Quantitative maturation sequences have not changed practice.
- Tunnel positions without graft signal would be a more useful report.

**5.7.10 Graft Maturation**

- Signal intensity ratios do not transfer between centres.
- Water content is at best indirectly related to mechanical strength.
- Quantitative composition methods do not measure stiffness.
- Ultra short echo time imaging is more direct and is not routine.
- An unvalidated surrogate is being pushed towards a return to sport decision.

**5.7.11 Graft Failure**

- The study's strongest measurement is its most inconsistently reported.
- Rotational failure is common and no routine study measures rotation.
- Tibial slope needs a lateral radiograph that is often not requested.
- Tunnel widening is a computed tomography question and is often skipped.
- The useful report names the tunnels, the slope and the corners.

**5.7.12 Tunnel Assessment**

- A position stated without its method is not a measurement.
- Accurate position needs reformatting the apertures are oblique to.
- Widening thresholds come from small series with different methods.
- The surgeon needs the position and the report supplies the signal.
- Automated tunnel geometry is tractable and has not arrived.

**5.7.13 Cyclops Lesions**

- The imaging appearance is commoner than the condition.
- No size or signal criterion separates symptomatic from incidental.
- The name describes an arthroscopic appearance, so the imaging call is inference.
- Recurrence relates to tunnel geometry the report often omits.
- The third question, whether the tunnel explains it, is rarely answered.

**5.7.14 Revision ACL Imaging**

- The needed information is spread across three studies with no convention binding them.
- The staging thresholds come from series with different measurement methods.
- The commonest failure mode is the least imaged.
- Tibial slope is accepted as a cause and its radiograph is not routine.
- Revision planning asks the most of imaging and gets the least organised answer.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.7.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
