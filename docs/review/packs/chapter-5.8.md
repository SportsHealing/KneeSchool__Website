## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.8.1 Normal PCL**

- Visibility creates a false sense of what the study settles.
- The referrer wants stability and the study shows continuity.
- The two answers coincide acutely and separate with time.
- Reliability runs opposite to the cruciate chapter's.
- The ligament's appearance is the least interesting part of its report.

**5.8.2 Partial Tears**

- The report sounds quantitative and is not.
- The grading language migrated from the examination to the scan.
- A graded scan stops the examination being repeated.
- Serial studies separate causes that one study cannot.
- The grading belongs to the examination.

**5.8.3 Complete Tears**

- The diagnosis is almost always right and almost never sufficient.
- The operation is decided by the pattern.
- The pattern is the part most often incomplete.
- A negative statement about the corner is worth as much as a positive one.
- The report names every structure it assessed with its state.

**5.8.4 Chronic PCL Injury**

- The appearance records history and the examination records competence.
- Healing in continuity makes the conflation possible.
- The same property explains the treatment and the reporting error.
- The cartilage reports function slowly and irreversibly.
- A chronic study records damage done rather than giving a warning.

**5.8.5 PCL Reconstruction**

- Two good outcomes can carry constructs that look nothing alike.
- An expected appearance exists only once the technique is known.
- The operation note is part of the study rather than background.
- A report written without it should say so.
- The pre-operative study records which structures were injured.

**5.8.6 Graft Assessment**

- Continuity and competence come apart in the graft as in the ligament.
- A native ligament heals in continuity and a graft can stretch.
- The baseline post-operative study is the most valuable in the series.
- Compartmental cartilage is the slow verdict on the operation.
- The laxity is not an imaging measurement.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.8.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
