## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**3.15.1 Football**

- Injury is a conjunction of movement, surface interaction and neuromuscular state.
- Removing any one prevents it, which is why prevention has targets and screening does not.
- Non contact injury occurs before voluntary correction is possible.
- Rotational traction decides how much angular momentum reaches the knee.
- Late match and late season presentations reflect fatigue rather than chance.

**3.15.2 Cricket**

- Workload management is the intervention here with a plausible dose response relationship.
- That evidence is largely lumbar, and the knee has been carried along with it.
- Low flexion at front foot contact keeps patellofemoral contact area small and pressure high.
- Patellar tendinopathy and fat pad irritation are characteristic.
- Adolescent bowlers carry disproportionate injury burden.

**3.15.3 Rugby**

- The question shifts from which ligament to how many.
- A multiligament knee is a different clinical entity with different urgency.
- Varus and hyperextension mechanisms put the peroneal nerve and popliteal vessels at risk.
- Knee dislocation is rare and limb threatening, and its vascular assessment cannot wait.
- Contact mechanisms are inferred from video and sectioning, not measured.

**3.15.4 Tennis**

- Traction is a property of the shoe and surface pair, and it is rarely in a history.
- Sliding lowers peak knee moment, which is the mechanical account of clay's reputation.
- The trade is volume: longer rallies raise cumulative load as peak load falls.
- Serve landings produce the sport's characteristic asymmetry.
- The surface argument rests on joint moments, not on measured tissue stress.

**3.15.5 Basketball**

- Jumps are countable, which makes the dose measurable and the problem tractable.
- Patellar tendon force at takeoff is among the highest the extensor mechanism sees.
- Tendinopathy localises where modelling predicts stress concentration.
- Court hardness alters loading rate rather than total load.
- Jump count is a proxy; tendon stress is not measured.

**3.15.6 Skiing**

- A technological change moved this sport's injury pattern to the knee.
- Modern boots nearly eliminated tibial fractures; the energy moved up.
- The phantom foot position is an efficient cruciate loading configuration.
- Boot induced anterior drawer loads the cruciate without rotation.
- Medial collateral injury from edge catching is commoner than cruciate injury.

**3.15.7 Athletics**

- Athletics is the natural experiment for the load versus repetition axis.
- Recreational running is not established as a risk factor for knee osteoarthritis.
- Jumping events concentrate patellar tendon load through a single leg takeoff.
- Sprint and hurdle events are dominated by hamstring rather than knee injury.
- Elite athletes are selected survivors of their own training, which biases the findings.

**3.15.8 Cycling**

- The intervention here is engineering rather than biology.
- Saddle height determines peak flexion and therefore patellofemoral force.
- Excessive height produces posterior pain and pelvic rocking.
- Restricted float imposes a rotation the knee did not choose.
- This sport has better individual biomechanical measurement than any other here.

**3.15.9 Golf**

- The mechanical modifications here are the easiest to make and the least often suggested.
- Lead knee internal rotation torque peaks through the downswing and impact.
- Opening the lead foot reduces the rotation the knee must produce.
- Restricted lead hip internal rotation transfers demand to the knee.
- Abundant swing measurement exists and is rarely framed as a knee question.

**3.15.10 Dance**

- The demand is set by an aesthetic rather than by a physical objective.
- That removes the argument that a safer technique is also a better one.
- Compensated turnout holds the tibia externally rotated through the whole squat cycle.
- Grand plié loads the proximal patella and odd facet, rarely loaded elsewhere.
- Vocational training through childhood makes the stakes of a restriction unusually high.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-3.15.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
