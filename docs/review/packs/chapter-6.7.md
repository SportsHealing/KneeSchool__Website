## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**6.7.1 Skeletally Immature Patients**

- The person consenting is not the person being operated on.
- A parent weighs decades and an adolescent weighs a season.
- Skeletal age and remaining growth is the variable the decision turns on.
- Demand is set by a squad, a school and a parent's ambition.
- Ask the child separately what they want.

**6.7.2 Physeal Injuries**

- One risk appears in a complaint and the other does not.
- Families and surgeons both weigh them asymmetrically.
- Demand determines how soon the alternative risk is realised.
- A frightened family hears a growth risk as a reason to do nothing.
- State both risks in the same sentence rather than in sequence.

**6.7.3 Reconstruction Strategies**

- Part of the choice is about the surgeon rather than the child.
- Sparing strategies are done well by those who do them often.
- The evidence favoured strategy and the reliable local one can differ.
- Families arrive wanting a named technique found online.
- Say which part of the choice is about the knee and which about the surgeon.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-6.7.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
