## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.17.1 ACL Reconstruction**

- The technique is rarely identifiable from the images.
- The index imaging is the most useful study to obtain.
- A second failure points away from the graft.
- Automated measurement looks settled and is not.
- The report gives the interval first.

**5.17.2 Meniscal Repair**

- Tissue quality decides and imaging cannot judge it.
- An untreated root tear is the commonest mechanical cause.
- Alignment belongs in every failed meniscal assessment.
- Serial scans compare protocols as much as healing.
- The report states that healing cannot be assessed.

**5.17.3 Root Repair**

- Failure and overload cannot be separated by imaging.
- The pre-operative extrusion value is the useful number.
- Insufficiency fracture is a known course.
- An uncorrected limb repeats the failure.
- The report carries the convention.

**5.17.4 Osteotomy**

- Three studies are needed and two are often missing.
- A corrected limb changes every later reference.
- Serial films across departments compare radiography.
- A corrected knee is harder to replace.
- The report names the method of measurement.

**5.17.5 Cartilage Procedures**

- Without the procedure the tissue cannot be classified.
- A second procedure changes the expectation again.
- Index imaging beats any new sequence.
- Automated grading is applied outside its training.
- The report names the procedure and the interval.

**5.17.6 MPFL Reconstruction**

- The technique is rarely identifiable and graft paths differ.
- The pre-operative measurements are the key record.
- A procedure on the groove makes its measurements describe the operation.
- Loaded studies address the real question and are scarce.
- The report states that stability is a clinical finding.

**5.17.7 Partial Knee Replacement**

- A serial comparison needs one projection.
- Repeating the series properly is often the first act.
- The conversion is planned from these films.
- Infection and loosening present alike.
- Metabolic imaging cannot separate them.

**5.17.8 Total Knee Replacement**

- Without a baseline the series cannot report loosening.
- Obtaining the first film beats any new study.
- Rotation on a replaced knee describes the operation.
- Metabolic imaging cannot separate the two causes.
- A revision starts a new baseline.

**5.17.9 Revision Arthroplasty**

- Identifying components from images is unreliable.
- Obtaining the records is part of the imaging task.
- Serial films across departments compare radiography.
- Metabolic imaging cannot answer the infection question.
- The deciding facts come from theatre and laboratory.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.17.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
