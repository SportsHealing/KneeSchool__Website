## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**5.10.1 LCL Injuries**

- The rotational structures are deeper and smaller.
- Their absence from a report carries no information.
- Varus alignment loads a lateral reconstruction.
- The examination and the scan use different vocabularies.
- The report should list every lateral structure.

**5.10.2 Popliteus Injuries**

- The structure is reported without its role.
- A reconstruction may have altered its path.
- A hypermobile meniscus explains a normal report.
- Dynamic ultrasound is operator dependent.
- The report should name the site along the tendon.

**5.10.3 PLC Injuries**

- The original scan usually shows what was not reported.
- A chronic injury may have no identifiable structures.
- Varus alignment is part of the problem and the operation.
- A rotational profile adds to a mismatched picture.
- The report lists components with their state.

**5.10.4 Popliteofibular Ligament**

- Most often absent from the report and most often relevant.
- Laxity with a normal looking lateral side is common.
- Re-read the original study for the styloid.
- A graft carries the function and not the structure.
- Cadaveric work defines its role better than imaging.

**5.10.5 Common Peroneal Nerve**

- Recovery is a clinical and electrophysiological question.
- A documented pre-operative deficit protects everyone.
- Serial muscle appearance tracks denervation.
- A ganglion within the nerve is treatable and missed.
- The nerve belongs in every lateral injury report.

**5.10.6 Post-operative PLC Imaging**

- Without the technique the construct cannot be judged.
- The pre-operative study records what was injured.
- Uncorrected varus stretches these reconstructions.
- The dial test reports what imaging cannot.
- The report names every tunnel and its convergence.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.10.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
