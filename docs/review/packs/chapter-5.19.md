**5.19.1 Physics of Magic Angle Imaging**

- The error is solved and the measurement is not.

- Information not recorded at acquisition cannot be recovered later.
- Ultra short echo time imaging changes the effect's purpose.
- An orientation dependent appearance needs the orientation recorded.

**5.19.2 Collagen Architecture**

**5.19.3 Tendon Imaging**

- The error is solved and still common, which locates it in practice.
- The affected tendons are the ones a corner reconstruction depends on.
- Both the false positive and the false negative cost an operation.
- After an operation the affected site becomes technique specific.

**5.19.4 Ligament Imaging**

- A ligament's variation along its length is real, not artefactual.
- The effect stands out less against a varying background.
- A graft's affected segment moves with the tunnels.
- A well functioning graft is reported abnormal from an unrecorded angle.
- The useful measurement would be fibre order within the graft.

**5.19.5 Meniscal Imaging**

- A repaired meniscus holds fibres at a new orientation.

**5.19.6 Cartilage Applications**

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-5.19.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
