## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**4.23.1 Examination Under Anaesthesia**

- It measures the knee the patient does not use.
- Grading by hand means two surgeons record different grades.
- The contralateral comparison fails when both knees are affected.
- Anaesthetic depth is an unrecorded variable.
- The findings cannot be repeated, so a disputed result has no appeal.

**4.23.2 Elite Athletes**

- Sport produces real asymmetry, so the dominant limb is not a control.
- The better the athlete, the less the examination detects.
- Functional thresholds come from non elite populations.
- The decision has interested parties beyond the patient.
- The examination contributes nothing to weighing a career against a season.

**4.23.3 Hypermobile Patients**

- Laxity grades are defined against a normal this patient does not have.
- The contralateral comparison fails, removing the usual fallback.
- The hypermobility threshold is a convention rather than a boundary.
- A connective tissue disorder changes surgical risk and is not found at the knee.
- Control and structural contributions are not apportionable here.

**4.23.4 Obese Patients**

- A negative finding carries less information and the record rarely says so.
- Clinical alignment is least reliable where it matters most.
- The functional record is proportionally more important here.
- The knee's contribution to reduced walking is harder to isolate.
- The soft tissue envelope is part of the assessment.

**4.23.5 Revision Surgery Patients**

- There is no valid normal for either knee, so only a change is interpretable.
- Pain has more candidate sources than the examination can separate.
- The history the findings are read against is itself reconstructed.
- Expectation after repeated disappointment predicts the reported result.
- The decision is often whether to stop operating.

**4.23.6 Complex Multi-ligament Injuries**

- The acute examination triages and the definitive one is deferred.
- The pattern is inferred from a set of individually unreliable findings.
- Vascular status changes over hours, so serial examination is the standard.
- The initial nerve examination sets the baseline for every later one.
- The pattern is operatively defined and clinically approximated.

**4.23.7 Medico-legal Assessment**

- Grades, end points and correctability are judgements by hand.
- The examination cannot date a finding.
- Effort dependent findings are noted rather than adjudicated.
- Stated uncertainty is a strength of the document.
- The limits are the clinic's limits, written down.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-4.23.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
