**4.24.1 Lachman Literature**

- The position is settled and the grading is not.
- Measured laxity and symptomatic instability remain loosely related.
- The partial tear is still clinically indistinguishable.
- Serial examination of one knee is a use the comparative work did not study.
- Chapter 3.17 carries the biomechanical literature behind this page.

**4.24.2 Pivot Shift Literature**

- A decision rests on the least reproducible quantity in the section.
- The post reconstruction shift inherits the same problem.
- Residual shift and reported instability remain loosely related.
- Reproducible measurement requires the knee the patient does not use.
- Chapter 3.17 carries the mechanics behind the finding.

**4.24.3 Meniscal Test Literature**

- Validation in knees selected for arthroscopy inflates apparent performance.
- A positive test in a worn knee can answer a question nobody asked.
- The classical tests do not detect a root tear reliably.
- Extrusion is the consequence that matters and is clinically undetectable.
- This chapter's value is largely a warning.

**4.24.4 Patellofemoral Literature**

- This is the section's clearest example of imaging displacing an examination.
- Clinical inference of trochlear shape is unreliable.
- Torsional contributions are assessed less often than the coronal ones.
- Anterior pain remains a diagnosis of exclusion.
- Chapter 3.17 carries the mechanics behind the shape argument.

**4.24.5 Functional Testing Literature**

- Criteria describe a moment and cohorts describe years.
- Thresholds are conventions from specific cohorts.
- Symmetry against a changed limb is the shared methodological weakness.
- Planned tasks differ from the loading that injures knees.
- Measurement of readiness is better established than the intervention for it.

**4.24.6 Future Directions in Knee Examination**

- Findings and judgements are recorded differently.
- A decision resting on an unreproducible grade is identified as such.
- A negative conclusion honestly recorded beats an unusable positive one.
- The serial record is the strongest product and the least often kept.
- This repository's registers are the honest reading list for its own pages.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-4.24.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.

## The same pack in two other formats

A spreadsheet, which is easier to fill, and a Word file, which is easier to print:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --chapter 4.24 --format all

All three carry the same rows and the same references, and `--ingest` reads any of them.
