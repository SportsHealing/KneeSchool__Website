## Claims to confirm

The deepest tier's key learning points on each page, which are the chapter's substantive claims. Every other statement in the body needs the same check; these are where to start.

**2.10.1 LCL**

- The lamina split in layer three is the usable lateral plane and makes two structure reconstruction possible.
- Conventional teaching separates the two femoral footprint centres by about 18 mm.
- This ligament controls varus; the other two primary structures control rotation.
- A fibular tunnel passes through a field the biceps tendon occupies, with the nerve behind it.
- Chronic lateral injury favours reconstruction, because lateral vascularity is poorer than medial.

**2.10.2 Popliteus Tendon**

- Eighteen millimetres on the surface is not 18 mm along a tunnel.
- The tendon crosses deep to superficial relative to the collateral ligament.
- The complex restrains rotation; the collateral ligament restrains the coronal plane.
- A graft restores the tendon and not the muscle that tensions it.
- The hiatus is both a meniscal defect and a route to the posterolateral compartment.

**2.10.3 Popliteofibular Ligament**

- The fibula carries three attachments and a nerve in one small field.
- There is no clean proximal border, so reattaching it tensions the popliteus tendon.
- Rotation restraint belongs to this ligament first and the tendon second.
- A few millimetres of position error is a large proportion of a 10 mm ligament.
- A lateral construct is judged on whether it crosses the fibula as well as the femur.

**2.10.4 Posterolateral Corner**

- Alignment is asked before the ligament question, not after a graft fails.
- Three structures give two findings, so the stage is an inference the exposure must exceed.
- A fibular tunnel must miss three attachments and the nerve behind them.
- Femoral convergence is three dimensional; the 18 mm surface measurement does not describe it.
- The nerve decides the approach, not the ligaments.

**2.10.5 Biceps Femoris**

- The tendon is the fixed lateral reference and survives injuries that destroy the ligaments.
- Its relationship to the nerve does not change with injury.
- An avulsed insertion removes the stabiliser and the landmark together.
- A peroneal palsy weakens the lateral dynamic stabiliser as well as the foot.
- The interval beside the anterior arm gives the fibular attachment without detaching it.

**2.10.6 Common Peroneal Nerve**

- The common thread across every operation at risk is a fixation or passage within 2 cm of a nerve that cannot move.
- Three anatomical facts compound: on bone, tethered in a tunnel, segmentally supplied.
- Follow the posteromedial border of biceps femoris; that relationship survives scar and swelling.
- Traction is the commonest injury and the quietest, because nothing happens at the time.
- Flexion relaxes the nerve and is available at any moment without changing the plan.

**2.10.7 Surgical Anatomy**

- The approach follows what is being reconstructed: ligament alone, or ligament and corner.
- In a revision the landmarks remain and the lamina split usually does not.
- The lateral condyle is asked to hold more tunnels than the medial, so convergence is planned first.
- A revision nerve sits in scar; start proximally in tissue nobody has entered.
- The lateral construct has to be right first time, because the lateral side does not repair.

## How to return this

1. Fill the Decision column on any row you have an answer for. Leave the rest blank; a blank row stays outstanding and comes back in the next pack.
2. Put your name in the Reviewer column, and a source in the Source column for a measurement.
3. Send the file back, then run:

       python3 tools/review_pack.py --ingest docs/review/packs/chapter-2.10.md

That writes each decision into the page's own register. The style gate then enforces it: a page carrying a recommendation that is not in its register fails POS-001, and a figure that is not in its register fails FIG-002, so a signed chapter cannot drift back to unsigned without the gate saying so.

Amending a sentence or correcting a figure needs the page edited as well as the register. Put the replacement wording in the Decision cell after `AMENDED:` or `CORRECTED:` and it is carried into the register's note field for whoever makes the edit.
