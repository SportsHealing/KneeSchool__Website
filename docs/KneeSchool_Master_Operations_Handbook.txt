KneeSchool Master Operations Handbook
1. Executive Summary
Purpose
KneeSchool.com is intended to become the definitive digital education platform for anyone who wants to understand the
knee. The platform is not a clinic brochure. It is an encyclopaedia, learning pathway, case library, video academy,
examination resource and research hub. It should serve patients, undergraduate medical students, postgraduate
trainees, FRCS candidates, fellows and advanced knee consultants.
Strategic Role
The platform should educate first, build trust second, and support commercial conversion third. It will create authority for
the founder, generate appropriate clinical referrals to Sportshealing, and provide contextual product pathways to
OmKneeHealth.
Operating Model
The content engine will use curated source material, AI-assisted drafting, assistant-led organisation, evidence verification
and consultant-level quality control. The assistant does not need to be a knee expert, but must follow strict source,
prompt, editorial and verification procedures.
Success Definition
Success means creating a large, accurate, searchable and trusted knee knowledge platform that users return to
repeatedly. It should feel closer to a specialist university and modern learning platform than a conventional healthcare
website.

2. Vision, Mission and Strategic Positioning
Vision
To build the world’s most useful and authoritative online knee education ecosystem, from basic patient education to
advanced fellowship and consultant-level knee knowledge.
Mission
To make knee knowledge clear, structured, evidence-based and accessible to every learner level while preserving clinical

accuracy and academic credibility.
Positioning Statement
KneeSchool is the complete knee knowledge hub: an educational platform for patients, students, trainees, fellows,
consultants, therapists and researchers.
Brand Promise
Learn the knee properly. Understand the science. Apply the knowledge safely.
Tone
Expert, concise, clear, educational, evidence-led, visually engaging and clinically responsible.
Not the Brand
Not a generic orthopaedic blog. Not a supplement sales site. Not a hospital marketing site. Not an unstructured archive of

articles. Not a superficial patient leaflet library.

5. Encyclopaedia Taxonomy
Anatomy
Bones, joints, cartilage, menisci, cruciate ligaments, collateral ligaments, posteromedial structures, posterolateral corner,
anterolateral structures, extensor mechanism, tendons, bursae, synovium, neurovascular anatomy and surgical anatomy.
Biomechanics
Load transmission, contact pressure, kinematics, meniscal hoop stress, ligament restraint patterns, patellofemoral
tracking, rotational stability, gait, sporting biomechanics and injury mechanisms.
Clinical Examination
History, inspection, palpation, range of motion, effusion, gait, ligament tests, meniscal tests, patellofemoral tests,
paediatric assessment and complex knee examination.
Imaging
X-ray, ultrasound, MRI, CT, alignment imaging, advanced imaging, AI in imaging, weight-bearing imaging and Magic
Angle MRI.
Conditions
Ligament injury, meniscal disorders, cartilage disorders, patellofemoral disorders, osteoarthritis, paediatric conditions,
inflammatory disorders, tendon disorders, tumours and infection.
Surgery
Arthroscopy, meniscal repair and transplantation, ACL, PCL, MCL, PLC, ALL, osteotomy, cartilage restoration, MPFL,
trochleoplasty, partial knee replacement, total knee replacement and revision surgery.
Rehabilitation
Principles, condition-specific rehabilitation, post-operative protocols, return to sport, return to work, strength, mobility, load

management and recovery.

6. Website Style Aesthetic and User Experience
Design Aim
The website should combine the authority of a medical school, the usability of a learning platform and the visual appeal of

a premium technology brand.
Colour Direction
Primary deep forest green, warm ivory background, restrained brushed gold accents, muted teal for learning highlights,
soft grey for structure.
Homepage Principle
The homepage must be search-first. A user should instantly understand that this is where to learn anything about the
knee.
Core Interactive Features
Large central search bar, learning pathway cards, interactive knee model, normal-versus-pathology imaging sliders, quiz
widgets, case challenges and progress tracking.
Page Design
Short sections, collapsible advanced content, summary boxes, key learning points, images, diagrams, videos, self-
assessment and related links. Avoid long unbroken textbook-style prose.
Mobile-First Requirement
Most users will access pages on mobile devices. Every page must be readable in short vertical sections, with clear
headings, large images and minimal visual clutter.

7. Content Production SOP
Operating Principle
The assistant’s role is knowledge management, source organisation, prompt execution, editorial review and evidence
verification. The consultant’s role is high-value clinical review.
Sequential Workflow
Understand site structure; select topic; gather sources; organise repository; upload to AI workspace; create content brief;
generate draft; run editorial checks; verify factual claims; prepare consultant review pack; complete SEO; publish.
Topic Selection
Prioritise foundational and high-search topics first: ACL, meniscus, osteoarthritis, knee pain, MRI, anatomy, examination
and rehabilitation.
Drafting Rule
AI output is a first draft only. It must never be published without structured quality control.
Version Control
Every article must have a draft version, checked version, consultant-reviewed version and published version.

8. Assistant Training Manual
First Week Training
Read the sitemap, understand the learner levels, review the article template, learn the folder structure, practise source
collection and produce three supervised sample drafts.
Core Responsibilities
Maintain source repository, track topics, upload sources, run prompts, format output, check references, highlight
uncertainty, prepare review packs and update publication tracker.
Boundaries
The assistant should not independently change clinical conclusions, invent missing references, rewrite complex surgical
nuance without review, or publish unreviewed material.
Daily Checklist
Open content tracker; confirm topic priority; gather sources; update repository metadata; create draft; check citations; log
uncertainties; prepare review package.
Escalation Triggers
Conflicting evidence, unclear surgical recommendation, unsupported statistic, patient safety issue, commercial claim,
drug or supplement claim, or any statement that could be construed as personal medical advice.

KneeSchool Master Operations Handbook
9. Source Acquisition SOP
Source Categories
Textbooks, review articles, landmark papers, guidelines, consensus statements, society resources, lecture slides, surgical

technique notes, internal research, imaging examples and video assets.
Priority Sources
Own publications and presentations; BASK resources; ESSKA resources; ISAKOS resources; PubMed papers; Cochrane

reviews; major textbook chapters; high-quality imaging teaching material.
Source Metadata
Every source must be logged with title, author, year, source type, topic, file location, relevance, quality level and notes.
Copyright Rule
Sources are for internal knowledge generation and review. Do not reproduce copyrighted text, figures or tables without
permission. Use paraphrasing and original diagrams where possible.
Minimum Source Standard
Each substantial article should have at least one textbook or review source, several peer-reviewed papers where relevant

and clear identification of any expert opinion.

10. AI Knowledge Base and Upload Workflow
Topic-Specific Uploads
Do not upload the entire library at once. Build focused knowledge bases such as ACL, Meniscus, Patellofemoral,
Osteotomy or Arthroplasty.
Upload Pack
For each topic, upload the source summary, key papers, textbook excerpts where licensed, internal slides, relevant
images and the article brief.
Context Discipline
The AI engine should be instructed to use only uploaded or specified sources for technical claims and to identify
uncertainty rather than invent missing details.
Output Control
Request structured output aligned to KneeSchool templates. Ask for citations and source mapping.
Post-Generation
Export the draft into the article tracker folder and mark it as AI draft, not final copy.

11. AI Prompt Library
Prompt Philosophy
Prompts should specify role, topic, audience, source constraints, structure, tone, output format, citations, safety
boundaries and commercial restrictions.
Universal Article Prompt
Use for standard encyclopaedia pages covering a topic across all learner levels.
Patient Page Prompt
Use for plain-language patient education. Avoid jargon, avoid diagnosis certainty, avoid direct personal medical advice
and include when to seek clinical assessment.
Trainee Prompt
Use for MRCS and FRCS content. Emphasise applied anatomy, examination, imaging, differential diagnosis,
management options, evidence and viva reasoning.
Fellowship Prompt
Use for advanced surgical topics. Include indications, contraindications, technique options, pitfalls, complications, failure
analysis, rehabilitation and controversies.
Case Prompt
Use for imaging or clinical cases. Include presentation, images to insert, questions, model answer, teaching points and
level.
Quiz Prompt
Use for MCQs, SBAs and viva questions with answer explanation and references.
SEO Prompt
Use after clinical approval to generate title, meta description, FAQ, schema suggestions and internal links.
Reusable Prompt Examples
Universal encyclopaedia page prompt:
You are an expert knee surgeon, anatomy teacher and medical education writer. Generate a
KneeSchool page on [TOPIC] for Patient, Medical Student, MRCS, FRCS and Fellowship levels.
Use UK English. Use only the provided sources for factual claims. Include overview, key
learning points, anatomy, biomechanics, clinical relevance, imaging, management,
rehabilitation, controversies, references, FAQs and related page suggestions. Identify
uncertainty rather than inventing facts.
Patient page prompt:
Explain [TOPIC] to an educated patient. Use clear language, avoid unnecessary jargon, explain

symptoms, diagnosis, treatment options, recovery, red flags and when to seek specialist
assessment. Do not provide individual medical advice. Include FAQs and links to related
KneeSchool pages.
FRCS viva prompt:
Create an FRCS viva preparation page on [TOPIC]. Include applied anatomy, classification,
examination, imaging, management algorithm, operative options, complications, evidence
controversies, common examiner questions and model answers.

12. Editorial Standards Manual
Language
Use UK English. Write in clear, direct prose. Avoid exaggerated claims. Avoid unsupported marketing language.
Medical Safety
Patient-facing pages must explain that information is educational and not a substitute for professional assessment.
Terminology
Use full terms on first use and abbreviations thereafter. Keep terminology consistent across the site.
Structure
Use predictable headings and short paragraphs. Each page should have overview, key learning points, main content,
FAQs, references and related pages.
References
Do not allow fabricated references. Every named study, guideline or statistic must be checked.
Commercial Tone
Commercial links must be contextual and educational, not intrusive. Education must remain primary.

13. Quality Assurance Manual
QA Tiers
Tier 1 AI generation; Tier 2 assistant editorial review; Tier 3 source verification; Tier 4 consultant review; Tier 5 publication

review.
Assistant QA
Checks structure, spelling, headings, references, internal links, duplication, clarity and formatting.
Evidence QA
Checks every factual claim that could affect clinical understanding. Statistics, surgical indications and treatment claims
require source support.
Consultant QA
Focuses on accuracy, nuance, omissions, controversies, clinical judgement, surgical pearls and safety.
Publication QA
Checks formatting, SEO, links, images, alt text, disclaimers, metadata and page status.
Red Flags
Unsupported statistics, overconfident claims, non-evidence-based supplement claims, incorrect anatomy, wrong
examination interpretation, outdated surgical recommendations or fabricated citations.

14. SEO and Internal Linking Manual
SEO Principle
Search visibility should be achieved through depth, clarity, topic authority and structured internal linking rather than
keyword stuffing.
Topic Clusters
Build clusters around ACL, meniscus, arthritis, patellofemoral pain, knee replacement, MRI, rehabilitation and paediatric
knee.
Each Page Requires
SEO title, meta description, slug, H1, H2 structure, FAQs, related articles, internal links and image alt text.
Internal Links
Every page should link upwards to its parent category, sideways to related topics and downwards to more advanced
pages.
Commercial Links
Only use commercial links where clinically relevant. For example, an osteoarthritis page may link to consultation, imaging,

rehabilitation and supplement education.
Content Refresh
High-value clinical pages should be reviewed every 6-12 months, or sooner if guidelines or major evidence changes.

KneeSchool Master Operations Handbook
15. Image and Video Standards
Visual Purpose
Images and video should clarify anatomy, pathology, imaging, surgical principles and rehabilitation. They should not be
decorative filler.
Image Categories
Anatomy diagrams, MRI examples, X-ray examples, ultrasound images, arthroscopy images, rehabilitation photographs,
surgical illustrations and infographics.
Video Categories
Patient explanations, clinical examination, imaging interpretation, surgical technique, rehabilitation exercise, expert
interviews and conference teaching.
Image Rights
Use original images where possible. For external figures, obtain permission or create original redrawn educational
diagrams.
Alt Text
Every image requires descriptive alt text for accessibility and SEO.
Quality Control
All clinical images must be anonymised and checked before upload.

KneeSchool Master Operations Handbook
16. Sportshealing Integration Strategy
Principle
Sportshealing should be presented as the clinical pathway for users who need expert assessment, imaging, diagnosis,
injections, surgery or rehabilitation.
Appropriate Placement
Condition pages, imaging pages, treatment pages, rehabilitation pages and complex case pages.
Call-To-Action Examples
Need a specialist knee assessment? Learn about Sportshealing consultations. Need imaging? Learn about MRI and
ultrasound pathways. Considering surgery? Learn about specialist knee surgical assessment.
Avoid
Aggressive sales language, excessive pop-ups, unsupported claims or implying all users need private care.
Conversion Assets
Consultation pages, imaging pathway, knee score test, patient questionnaires, outcome measures and educational
downloads.

KneeSchool Master Operations Handbook
17. OmKneeHealth Integration Strategy
Principle
OmKneeHealth should be integrated as a supportive musculoskeletal health and recovery product pathway, not as a
replacement for diagnosis or treatment.
Appropriate Placement
Nutrition, collagen, vitamin D, osteoarthritis, recovery, strength, women’s knee health, ageing knee and prevention pages.
Compliance Standard
Avoid disease treatment claims unless supported and legally compliant. Use cautious educational language.
Product Education
Explain ingredients, rationale, evidence level, cautions and who should seek medical advice before use.
Commercial Format
Use subtle product cards, related resources and education-led links rather than banner advertising.
