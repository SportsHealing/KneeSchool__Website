KneeSchool Master Playbook v1
Comprehensive strategic playbook created from the planning discussions prior to the academy architecture.
Executive Vision
KneeSchool will be a global digital university dedicated entirely to knee health. It will combine patient education, medical education, imaging, surgery, rehabilitation, performance science and research into a single ecosystem. The platform will support Sportshealing clinical services and OmKneeHealth products through education-led engagement.
