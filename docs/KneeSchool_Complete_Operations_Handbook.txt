KneeSchool Complete Operations Handbook
Executive Summary
KneeSchool is designed as the world's leading knee education ecosystem. It serves patients,
students, trainees, fellows and consultants while supporting Sportshealing and OmKneeHealth.
Vision and Mission
Create the definitive online encyclopaedia and curriculum for knee health, pathology, imaging,
surgery, rehabilitation and research.
Target Audiences
Patients; Medical Students; MRCS; FRCS; Fellows; Consultants; Allied Health Professionals.
Full Sitemap Framework
Home; Learn by Level; Patient Academy; Medical Student Academy; MRCS Academy; FRCS
Academy; Fellowship Academy; Anatomy; Biomechanics; Imaging; Conditions; Surgery;
Rehabilitation; Women's Knee Health; Children's Knee Health; Performance; Research; Cases;
Question Bank; Video Library; Products and Services; Community; About.
Educational Pathways
Each topic should be explained progressively from Patient level through Fellowship level.
Content Categories
Anatomy, Biomechanics, Examination, Imaging, Conditions, Surgery, Rehabilitation, Research,
Women's Health, Paediatric Knee, Performance and Prevention.
Website Design Standards
Search-first homepage, interactive anatomy, MRI sliders, progress tracking, mobile-first design,
premium academic appearance.
Source Acquisition SOP
Collect textbooks, society resources, landmark papers, guidelines, imaging examples,
presentations and internal teaching materials.
Repository Structure
Topic folders for ACL, PCL, MCL, PLC, Meniscus, Cartilage, Patellofemoral, Osteotomy,
Arthroplasty, Imaging, Rehabilitation and Research.
Assistant Training Manual
Learn sitemap; understand audience levels; collect sources; organise repositories; upload to AI;
generate briefs; run prompts; quality control; prepare consultant review.
Prompt Engineering Library
Use standardised prompts requiring UK English, evidence-based content, multiple educational
levels, references, FAQs, quizzes and internal links.


Article Template
Overview; Patient Section; Medical Student Section; MRCS Section; FRCS Section; Fellowship
Section; Key Learning Points; Imaging; Treatment; Rehabilitation; Controversies; References.
Editorial Standards
No unsupported claims. No fabricated references. Use UK spelling. Consistent heading hierarchy.
Quality Control SOP
Editorial review, source verification, consultant review, SEO review and publication approval.
SEO Framework
Keyword research, meta descriptions, FAQs, schema markup, internal links and topic clusters.
Sportshealing Integration
Contextual links to consultations, imaging, injections, surgery and rehabilitation.
OmKneeHealth Integration
Contextual links to supplements, braces and recovery products where appropriate.
Research Academy
Landmark papers, journal clubs, methodology, statistics and critical appraisal.
Case Library
Patient cases, MRCS cases, FRCS viva cases and fellowship-level challenges.
Video Academy
Anatomy, examination, imaging, rehabilitation and surgical videos.
KPIs
Monthly content targets, repository growth, publication rates, SEO performance and learner
engagement.
18-Month Roadmap
Foundation; Encyclopaedia Build; Surgical Academy; Research Academy; Video Academy; AI
Tutor.
