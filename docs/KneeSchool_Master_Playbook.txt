KneeSchool.com Master Development Playbook
Vision
KneeSchool is intended to become the world's leading educational platform dedicated to knee
health, anatomy, biomechanics, imaging, pathology, surgery, rehabilitation and research. It serves
patients, medical students, MRCS trainees, FRCS candidates, fellows and consultants. The
platform acts as a knee encyclopaedia while supporting Sportshealing clinical services and
OmKneeHealth products.
Educational Levels
Patient Academy; Medical Student Academy; MRCS Academy; FRCS (Tr & Orth) Academy;
Fellowship Academy; Consultant Masterclass.
Core Site Structure
Learn by Level; Knee Encyclopaedia; Anatomy; Biomechanics; Clinical Examination; Imaging;
Conditions; Surgery Academy; Rehabilitation Academy; Women's Knee Health; Children's Knee
Health; Performance & Prevention; Research Academy; Case Library; Question Bank; Video
Library; Knee Score Centre; Products & Services; Community; About.
Homepage Concept
Large search-first experience, learning pathways, interactive knee model, featured learning
modules, topic exploration cards, progress tracking and AI-assisted search.
Design Aesthetic
University-grade authority combined with modern educational design. Deep green, ivory, gold
accents and scientific visual identity. Mobile-first architecture. Interactive anatomy, imaging sliders,
quizzes and learning pathways.
Development Timeline
Phase 1 Foundation (Months 1-3); Phase 2 Educational Build (Months 4-9); Phase 3 Advanced
Content and Monetisation (Months 10-18). Create structure first, then core anatomy, biomechanics,
examination, conditions, surgery, research and multimedia libraries.
Content Production Targets
Month 3: 100 articles. Month 6: 250 articles. Month 12: 600 articles. Month 18: 1000+ articles.
Knowledge Sources
Textbooks: Scott Surgery of the Knee, Insall & Scott, DeLee & Drez, Campbell, Miller, Netter,
Gray's Anatomy, AAOS Basic Science, Nordin & Frankel. Societies: BASK, ESSKA, ISAKOS,
AAOS, AOSSM. Databases: PubMed, Cochrane, Google Scholar. Imaging resources: Radiopaedia
and institutional teaching files. Internal assets: publications, lectures, presentations, surgical notes,
videos and research.
Repository Structure
Create topic folders for ACL, PCL, MCL, PLC, Meniscus, Cartilage, Patellofemoral, Osteotomy,
Arthroplasty, Paediatric Knee, Rehabilitation, Women's Knee Health, Imaging and Research. Each


folder contains textbooks, landmark papers, guidelines, presentations, videos, images and draft
articles.
Assistant Workflow
1. Learn site map and educational levels. 2. Gather source material. 3. Organise repository. 4.
Upload topic-specific sources to AI. 5. Create content brief. 6. Generate article with AI. 7. Editorial
review. 8. Fact verification. 9. Consultant review. 10. SEO optimisation. 11. Publish.
AI Prompt Framework
Generate content for Patient, Medical Student, MRCS, FRCS and Fellowship levels. Include
anatomy, biomechanics, pathology, imaging, treatment, rehabilitation, controversies, references,
FAQs and key learning points. Use UK English and evidence-based language.
Quality Control
Tier 1 AI generation. Tier 2 assistant editorial review. Tier 3 evidence verification. Tier 4 consultant
review. Tier 5 publication. No fabricated references. All factual claims checked against source
material.
Weekly Operating Schedule
Monday: gather and organise sources. Tuesday: create briefs and AI drafts. Wednesday: editorial
review and fact checking. Thursday: prepare review packs. Friday: consultant review and
publication.
Commercial Integration
Educational content remains primary. Sportshealing consultations, imaging, injections, surgery and
rehabilitation are linked contextually. OmKneeHealth supplements, braces and recovery products
are suggested only where relevant.
Long-Term Vision
Develop the definitive global knee education ecosystem including encyclopaedia, learning
academy, AI tutor, case library, examination preparation, consultant masterclasses, video
academy, research hub and clinical referral pathways.
