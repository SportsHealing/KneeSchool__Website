What This Is

The Future Surgeon Challenge is a ladder. Each rung asks harder questions than the one below it, and the questions are drawn from the same material the site teaches at deeper levels.

The ladder is the clearest demonstration of what this site is built around. The same topic appears at every level, and what changes is the depth of the question rather than the subject.

The question bank the ladder runs on has not been built. It belongs to Section 14 of the publishing plan, which is not written yet. The ladder described below is therefore a design and not a live feature, and there is nothing to attempt on this page today.

What It Covers

Five rungs, each matched to one of the site's levels.

Rung 1, Junior. Chapters 0.1 and 0.2. What the parts are called and what they do.

Rung 2, Patient. Section 1 and the condition pages. What goes wrong, what it feels like, and what is usually done about it.

Rung 3, Medical Student. Sections 1 to 3. Anatomy by region, how the joint moves, and how a knee is examined.

Rung 4, MRCS. Sections 4 and 5. Diagnosis from history, examination and imaging.

Rung 5, FRCS and above. Sections 6 to 8. Management decisions, operations and the arguments the specialty has not settled.

A school student working through rung 1 and rung 2 is covering real material rather than a simplified version of it, which is the design the ladder is meant to show.

How to Earn It

The design is that you climb a rung by answering a set of questions correctly, and that wrong answers send you back to the page the question came from rather than simply being marked wrong.

That mechanism needs a question bank, a way of knowing which questions you have seen, and somewhere to keep your progress. The site currently has none of the three.

Until it does, the ladder can be climbed by hand. Read the section a rung covers, then ask somebody to question you on it. The Knee Explorer Certificate works this way already and rung 1 of this ladder is the same material.

Rules and Fair Play

When the ladder opens, these rules will apply.

Attempt rungs in order. Skipping to rung 4 without rungs 1 to 3 wastes your time rather than cheating anyone.

No time limits. Thinking slowly is not a fault and the questions are not a test of speed.

Looking something up is allowed. Finding the answer in the material is the skill being built.

No scores are published against anybody's name. See the School Leaderboards page for why that constraint exists.

Where to Find Out More

The level pages for Junior, Patient, Medical Student, MRCS and FRCS list what each rung covers.

The Knee Explorer Certificate is the part of this chapter that works today.

The build status page records what is missing before the ladder can open, so progress on it can be followed.

Key Learning Points

  • The challenge is a ladder of five rungs matched to the site's own levels.
  • The same topics appear at every rung, with the depth of the question changing.
  • The question bank belongs to Section 14 and has not been built, so nothing is playable yet.
  • Rung 1 can be done by hand today using the Knee Explorer Certificate questions.
  • No scores will be published against anyone's name.

If any of this sounds like something happening to you, speak to a parent, teacher, coach or doctor.
