For Young Learners

What This Is

The Knee Explorer Certificate recognises that you have worked through chapters 0.1 and 0.2 of the Junior Academy and can answer questions on them.

It is not a qualification and it does not count towards anything at school. It is a record that you learned something, which is worth having for its own sake and is worth mentioning if you later apply for work experience.

The certificate is awarded by a teacher, a coach or a parent rather than by this site, because the site has no accounts and cannot know who has read what.

What It Covers

Eleven pages across two chapters.

Chapter 0.1 Discover the Knee. What the knee is made of, how it moves, why it is built the way it is, how it differs while you are still growing, and how it maps onto GCSE and A level biology and physical education.

Chapter 0.2 Sport and Your Knees. The injuries that occur in young athletes, how warming up changes risk, why anterior cruciate ligament injury rates differ between girls and boys, and when a knee problem needs an adult told about it.

The certificate covers understanding, not memory. Nobody needs to recite the names of the ligaments to earn it.

How to Earn It

Four steps.

1. Read the eleven pages in chapters 0.1 and 0.2. They total around five thousand words, which is about forty minutes of reading.

2. Answer the five questions below out loud to the person awarding the certificate. The point is explaining rather than reciting, so answering in your own words counts and answering in the site's words does not count more.

Name the four bones that meet at or near the knee, and say what the kneecap does.

Explain why a knee can bend and straighten easily but should not twist far.

Say what a growth plate is and why a young knee is treated differently from an adult one.

Give two reasons female athletes tear the anterior cruciate ligament more often than male athletes.

List three knee symptoms that mean telling an adult straight away.

3. Describe one thing from the chapters that surprised you. There is no right answer and this is the question that shows whether the reading landed.

4. The adult awarding it signs and dates the certificate.

A printable certificate is not available to download yet. The page it will sit on is part of the classroom materials in chapter 0.7, and those files have not been produced.

Rules and Fair Play

Answer in your own words. Reading an answer off the screen while being asked defeats the only purpose the certificate has.

Getting a question wrong is not a failure. Go back to the page it came from and try again. There is no limit on attempts and no record kept of them.

Nobody has to earn this. It exists for people who want a marker of having finished something, and ignoring it entirely costs nothing.

Key Learning Points

  • The certificate covers the eleven pages of chapters 0.1 and 0.2.
  • It is awarded by a teacher, coach or parent, because the site keeps no accounts.
  • It tests explaining in your own words rather than reciting names.
  • There is no limit on attempts and no record is kept.
  • The printable certificate file does not exist yet.

If any of this sounds like something happening to you, speak to a parent, teacher, coach or doctor.
