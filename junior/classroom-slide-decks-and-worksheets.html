Curriculum Links

The planned materials follow the mapping on the Curriculum Links page, which covers GCSE and A level biology and physical education.

Each worksheet is intended to carry the specification reference for the topic it covers, so a deck can be dropped into an existing scheme of work.

What This Covers

The plan is a set of downloadable files to go with the lesson plans in 0.7.1: one slide deck per lesson, a worksheet, a diagram sheet and the printable Knee Explorer Certificate.

None of those files exists. Nothing on this page can be downloaded today, and saying otherwise would waste your preparation time.

What does exist is every piece of subject content the files would carry. It is on the pages this chapter links to, and it is free to read, project and link to.

How to Use It

Until the files are produced, three approaches work.

Project the site page. The pages are single column, print cleanly and reflow on a whiteboard without anything being cut off.

Use the key learning points as a worksheet. Every page ends with three to five of them, and turning each into a question takes a few minutes.

Use the certificate questions. The five questions on the Knee Explorer Certificate page cover chapters 0.1 and 0.2 and work as an end of topic assessment.

Pages may be printed and copied for classroom use. There is nothing to pay and nothing to register for.

What to Watch For

Check the status note at the top of any page before you set it. Pages marked as editorial drafts have not been through consultant review, and that is stated on the page itself rather than hidden.

No diagram on this site is a substitute for a clinical image, and none of the material should be used to interpret a particular pupil's knee.

If you build your own resource from these pages, the governance that applies to the Junior Academy is worth reading first. In particular, nothing in this chapter may be used in anything commercial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. When will the files be available? No date has been set. The build status page is where progress on them is recorded.

Q. Can I make my own slides from these pages? Yes, for teaching. The one restriction is that nothing from the Junior Academy may be used in anything commercial.

Q. Do I need an account to print a page? No. The site has no accounts and nothing on it is behind a login.

Q. Are the diagrams suitable for an examination class? They are teaching diagrams. Check them against your board's specification, because the labelling conventions examination boards expect are not always the clinical ones.

If a pupil raises a knee problem of their own after a lesson, the answer is to have it assessed by a doctor or physiotherapist. No material on this site can tell you what is wrong with a particular knee.
